# {{TITLE}} v0.8.0 — Fights and Feel
_2026-10-22_ · A final boss, bosses with a mechanic of their own, a balance pass on every build, and floors that look and run better.

## New Features
### The Hollow Crown
Ending the run at the Ascension portal now takes you to a final boss in its own cosmic arena. The Hollow Crown fights in three bodies: the
Colossus, the Prism and the Fallen. The Fallen is your own shadow and fights with your run's weapon. Beat all three
for the true ending.
- Each body lasts about two minutes against a starting build, and about one against the strongest.
- The Prism's second phase is fair: a slower Cross, three fixed orb patterns, one spell at a time.

### Every boss has its own trick
- The Gatekeeper leaves stone pillars to hide behind, and the Warlord's shield blocks hits from the front.
- The Hive Lens sweeps a turning beam, and the Siege Engine fires a turret cross.
- Every boss attack winds up, lands on the tick it hits, and leaves you a window to punish.

### Boss doors
Each biome's boss room has its own door, set into the wall like a gatehouse. It stays shut until you press interact
by it, then it opens to its own sound.

### Rooms worth fighting in
Every room size is furnished from hand-designed layouts. The shop is a merchant's stall in a room of its own, and the
curses live in a dark pact room. Each biome's walls are one modelled ruin in its own stone.

## Items
Cards were rebalanced for every build.
- **Stats:** damage bonuses add up instead of multiplying, and Glass Cannon multiplies the total.
- **Extra bolts and repeats:** they share the damage now (Twin 60 %, Spread 55 %, Echo 50 %).
- **Rarity:** cards come in four tiers, Common, Rare (now blue), Epic and Legendary, each named on the card.
- **Card order matters:** the Ring turns your shot into a flying lightning ring, and Spread and Swirl change it
  depending on where they sit.
- **New cards:** Threadline, Faultline and Gravity Stitch leave things behind for later attacks.
- **Chests deal one card.** Only altars let you choose 1 of 3.
- **Floor 1:** it favours forms and elements, and its first altars each offer a card that changes your attack.

## Enemies
- Each role has its own colour family: melee red-orange, ranged blue-violet, bombers yellow, support green, tanks
  bronze.
- Melee enemies no longer paint red marks on the ground; they crouch and glow before they strike.
- New enemy models, and enemies take the shortest way to you instead of standing against walls.
- Fights come in waves of intensity, then quiet.

## Combat
- The Gun's shot is bigger and hits harder, a little slower. The Blade swings with a full wind-up and follow-through.
- Every hit you land shows sparks, and the effects are tinted by element.
- Void leaves a small black hole that pulls enemies in.
- Each Ascension loop gives enemies ×6 HP and ×1.2 damage.

## Floors
- The hero arrives on a receiver at the start of each run, and every new run has its own seed.
- Loads never freeze the screen.
- Big fights cost far less to draw, with the same look. On high quality, fire light hangs in a low fog with drifting
  embers.

## User Interface
- Cards, the shop, the Codex and achievements are carved stone tablets.
- Reward cards deal face down and flip, showing what gets better (old → new).
- Health bars show the chunk you just lost, the heal and the shield.
- Tips name only the controls. The Playground moved into the developer panel (F10).

## Also in this build: New Ground (v0.7.5)
- Two new biomes: Frozen Shore and Starfall.
- The Ascension portal: end the run, or loop for harder floors.
- The Codex and local achievements.
- Bigger floors, up to 20 rooms.
