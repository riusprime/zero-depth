# Zero Depth v0.12.0 — Bosses You Learn
_2026-11-23_ · Seven bosses rebuilt around a mechanic you learn in every phase, a final boss that remembers your run and steals your best card, new models for every enemy, new props and hall centrepieces, and faster big fights.

## New Features
### Bosses you learn
Every reworked boss now asks you to do something in each phase: stand in the right place, use what it gives you, or turn its own attack against it. Missing one hurts a lot, but a single miss is not meant to kill you from full health. None of it is explained in words: arrows and glows point at what matters when you first meet it.
- **The Clockwork Judge** fights with time. Every 25 s its Time Wave takes 75 % of your attack speed; walk through its three hour orbs to win back 25 % each. From phase 2, stopped gears block the ways to the orbs, slow fields between you and the Judge slow your shots, and its Bell throws you back to where you stood 4 s ago, onto a Verdict square that drops a moment later: step off it. In phase 3 the orbs last only 10 s and the Bell tolls twice.
- **The Tidecaller** dances, one thing at a time with calm in between. Its Pull drags you in and its push throws you clear (and pops the enemy shots around you). From phase 2, Riptide floods three of four strips of the room: find the dry one. In the last phase it walks to the middle of the room and stays there; its whirlpool carries you round it, its eye throws you out, and High Tide floods everything but one dry circle.
- **The Twin Wardens** fight apart, with no chain between them. Amber is a fast swordsman: combos and an overhead up close, a Sword Lane from range, and a charge that comes with only a white-hot flash and aims where you are going. Violet raises a Mirror Ward that sends your shots back and blocks your swings, and cuts the floor with Shadow Swords that leave burning rifts. In phase 3 Violet hides under a whole ward: bait Amber's long charge through her to break it and stun her.
- **The Siege Engine** makes its own cover. From phase 2 a Target Lock follows you and lands a shell that wrecks the Engine's turrets beneath it and leaves a boulder. In phase 3 its Shockwave hits you anywhere it can see you, so hide behind a boulder. Ammo carts roll toward it: let one arrive and a Doom Shell falls unless you are in cover; destroy it next to the Engine and its armour comes off for a while.
- **The Foundry** forges a plate that cuts its damage taken by 60 %. Its own bomb drones throw at you: make a bomb land next to the Foundry to blow the plate off. In phase 3 its lava cools into slabs, Overpressure burns the open floor while the slabs stay safe, and you heat up with every shot and step in lava until you cool off on a slab.
- **The Warlord** lunges fast and the lunge bursts where it stops. In phase 2 it plants a War Banner: inside it the Warlord attacks faster and you hit it harder, and a lunge baited through the pole knocks the banner down. In phase 3 a javelin cage closes on you both: a duel with its shield down, then the javelins return through the centre.
- **The Hive Lens** is fed by its drones: each living drone makes it take less damage. Its Focus Beam kills any drone in its path, so put a drone between you and the Lens. In phase 3 the beam leaves burning glass, and in its Lattice the drones circle it, joined to it by strands of light; killing a drone cuts its strands.
- **The Gatekeeper's** shock lanes now run from its edge toward where you stood, three of them, and their spikes rise outward one slice at a time.
- Boss warnings and states no longer show text on the boss bar or over the boss; the fight shows them.

### The Fallen remembers
The Hollow Crown's last body no longer vanishes between its phases. It stays in the room and, at two thirds and one third of its health and every so often between, casts a mechanic of a boss you killed this run: the Judge's Time Wave and hour orbs, the Tidecaller's Riptide, the Gatekeeper's quake pillars, or the Twin Wardens' ward with an amber shade that charges at you. A ghost of that boss rises over it while it casts.
- **It steals your best card.** At two thirds and one third of its health your strongest card goes dark in its slot and The Fallen casts it at you. Hit the violet mark on its back eight times to win the oldest one back. Every card comes back when it dies.

### Enemies on new models
Every regular enemy, the Star Warper and the five horde critters now have new models of their own, animated: Hatchling, Swarmer, Needle, Sniper, Warden, Mine Layer, Arc Caster, Bomb Drone, Wire Weaver, Frost Spitter, Glacier Turtle and Gravity Node. Floating enemies (Bomb Drone, Star Warper, Arc Caster, Cave Bat, Star Mote) hover, bob and bank instead of walking.
- Each biome's own versions of the shared enemies wear that biome's colour.
- The Gravity Node now gently pulls you when you are within 5 m (never more than about half your run speed, and never your dash).
- The Warlord's javelins and War Banner, the Clockwork Judge's hour clocks and the Siege Engine's ammo cart are new models too.

### Rooms, props and halls
- **Room pieces:** the camps get a tent, log seats and a cook pot; the collapsed halls statues, a round column and fallen column drums; the quarries a crane, a mine cart, stone blocks and a tool rack. The Ruins add metal posts, dumpsters, cable spools and sign frames, and the Frozen Shore ice floes.
- **Props:** new crates, urns, barrels and an explosive barrel that bursts once, new hazards for every biome, the approach pylon, and a timed door built from the walls' own stone blocks, whose slab sinks to open.
- **Hall centrepieces:** a grand arch stands at full size in the Ruins and Night Rocks halls, and you can walk through its opening; a great monolith stands in Starfall. The observatory, fire pit and quarry rock face are larger.

### The summoned Blade
The Blade forms from runes for each swing and breaks into motes after it. It starts at your body, and its sparks travel with you as you move.

### Graphics options
Options has a resolution list (windowed and fullscreen) and a Graphics page: upscaler (with FSR 2), shadows, ambient occlusion, glow, anti-aliasing and texture filtering. The defaults look as before.

## Combat
### Boss style
Every boss effect was gone over to fit the game's look:
- Warnings crack the floor in dark lines instead of glowing lava.
- Stone hits break stone: craters, slams, landings and shells throw faceted debris instead of smoke columns.
- Bosses wind up with a rim in their own colour instead of turning red, and every boss model is matte, lit like the walls, with a soft light so it reads in every room.
- Hostile shots are faceted stones and gems; the Judge's gears are iron and gold; the Siege Engine's cart is a wooden wagon and its rail shot leaves a streak behind it as it slides.

### After the playtests
Two rounds of playtest feedback reached the Tidecaller's rhythm, the Twin Wardens, the Gatekeeper and the Foundry:
- **The Twin Wardens:** Amber chases you, and his charge in phase 1 comes every 10–15 s from any distance. Phase 2's Crossfire is a ring of shots with one marked gap close to you. Phase 3 starts without the ward, which Violet raises a few seconds in.
- **The Tidecaller:** its Pull, Riptide and High Tide take turns, never two at once, with at least 3 s of calm between. The dry Riptide strip is bare floor.
- **The Foundry:** its fireballs share one look, its slag fountains leave room to pass between shots, and its Furnace Waves' walls alternate.

## Performance
- **Big fights:** every enemy on a model is now drawn by the native batch. In a 42-enemy fight in Night Rocks a frame takes about 5 ms less (from about 54 to about 75 fps on the test PC, an i7-10700K with an RTX 3060), and 2–4 ms less in the Frozen Shore.
- **The processor:** the hero's cloak, idle views, the HUD rows, the sound director and the damage meter skip work that changes nothing. The picture is the same.

## Bug Fixes
- The corner minimap draws the rooms again.
- Blood Price offers only an ability that can still level up.
- Pack explosions play once (one blast used to read as two or three).
- Enemies leave no smoke behind, and Starfall's glowing pools fade out before their edges.
- Night Rocks enemies stand on the same dark disc as in every other biome.
- The long stalls in Night Rocks fights, the first time some effects were drawn, are fixed: those effects are now prepared behind the loading screen.
