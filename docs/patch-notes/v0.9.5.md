# {{TITLE}} v0.9.5 — Harder Bosses, Deeper Guns
_2026-10-26_ · Bosses that hit back, every Gun form takes the cards, a stone in the hub that talks, and a world that is easier to read.

## Bosses
- **No stagger.** Bosses can't be knocked out of their attacks. At each phase change a boss kneels for a second (you can hit it) and its core can be stolen for 3 s.
- **Their health is read at the door.** When the boss door starts to open, the boss's HP is set from how strong you are (capped, and a little different every fight).
- **One hard spell each.** The Gatekeeper's shock lanes lock faster and fire a second set. The Warlord's javelins lead you. The Brood Mother's third leap follows you. The Hive Lens's beam turns after you. The Siege Engine's mortar grid leaves two safe cells that swap halfway. The Foundry's furnace waves come from two sides. The Twin Wardens' crossfire tracks you. The Clockwork Judge adds a counter-turning spiral. The Lantern Shade's dark lasts longer and Snuff puts out two lanterns.
- **The Tidecaller** pulls and pushes harder, bubble rings fly all fight, and short waves form at the walls and roll across the room.
- **The Hollow Crown** is faster in its first body, its second body's ball patterns leave wider gaps and a longer window to hit back, and the Fallen now vanishes twice for 15 s of rays, ball rains or darkness before it slams back down.
- After you beat the Hollow Crown, each loop (up to 3) eases it a little. The run summary says "Looped x N".

## The Gun
### Rune Explosion
- **Rune Explosion replaces Scatter.** A violet rune dome grows out of you every 1.1 s, hits each enemy once and shoves it. Twin adds a second, smaller burst.

### Every form takes the cards
Homing, Halo, Ring, Backfire, Ricochet, Echo, Big Shot, Rapid Fire, Split, Mine and the rest now do something on every Gun form. The best fits:
- **Laser:** Homing (with Pierce the beam threads the 5 nearest enemies), Halo, Echo.
- **Bomb:** Halo (four bombs in a cross), Ring, Echo.
- **Void:** Ring (it collapses in lightning), Homing, Big Shot.
- **Lance:** Split, Ring, Echo.
- **Rune Explosion:** Ring, Twin, Big Shot.
- **Glaive:** Echo, Ring, Mine.
- **Seekers:** Pierce, Echo, Ring.
- **Wave:** Big Shot, Echo, Ring.
- **Wisps:** Big Shot, Echo, Halo.
- Each card now shows one line for the form you hold, or "Does nothing on this form".

### Card levels and heat
- Cards level only where it shows. 23 Gun cards (Pierce, Homing, Ricochet ...) are offered once with a fixed value. Twin, Halo, Split and the elements have 3 levels. Form cards don't level.
- The Pulse Gun is no longer offered; damage comes from your stat cards.
- **Heat** fills with the time you spend firing (0 to 100 in 14 s, whatever the form), and cools 1 s after you stop. Vent needs 40 heat and has a 6 s cooldown.
- **Wisps** hit harder (80 % of an orb, up from 40 %) and pierce one enemy.
- **Stress Mark is rebuilt.** Nearby enemies show an orange arc on one side. Hit them from that side for double damage, once; the mark comes back after 8 s on a new side.

### Elements
- Fire, Lightning, Frost, Venom and Bleed have 3 levels, each with a reward (Venom spreads, Fire burns wider, Frost shatters ...).
- Two level 3 elements wake a named pair: Thermal Shock, Toxic Fumes, Ignition Arc, Boiling Blood, Cold Circuit, Black Ice, Brittle, Neurotoxin, Arterial Arc and Sepsis.

### Retired
- Gravity Stitch, Double Exposure, Grand Finale and the On Hit, On Kill, On End and On Finisher cards are gone. Saves that held them are refunded in shards.

## The world
- **Two kinds of sealed room:** the locked room (one burst of enemies and a free altar) and the Overrun (waves and an extra reward). Every seal shows iron bars; the Overrun's doorway has a red rune on the floor.
- **Fewer event altars:** about 30 % of floors, twice a run at most, never the same one twice.
- **Timed doors:** the two switches sit in different rooms near the door, the timer is 20 s, and a lever inside the secret room reopens the door for 5 s.
- **Solid things are solid.** Altars, chests, pylons, pedestals, rune pillars, crates, urns and barrels push you and enemies. Shots pass over them. Crates never block a doorway. Bosses' bodies fit their models.
- **Backgrounds** for every biome: a deep sky or sea that drifts behind the floor.
- **Danger keeps rising** to minute 10 and past it, instead of stopping at minute 5.
- **Chests you can refuse:** Esc or B closes a dealt card and it waits for you.
- Additive effects (portals, the laser's tip) no longer show a lit square in foggy biomes. Enemies are no longer drawn through walls.

## Interface
- **Four big ability squares** bottom-right (Attack, Skill, Dash, Blink) with a falling cooldown and seconds. The HP plate is 15 % bigger. The small blue trackers are gone.
- **Skill is on L2 / LT** on a controller.
- **Short prompts** under the hero: a drawn button and a verb, and a cost only when there is one (red when you can't pay). Controller buttons show up when you play with a pad.
- **A cursor:** a rune reticle in runs and a rune arrow in menus; it hides while a pad plays.
- **Shrines are clearer:** a failing shrine flashes, cracks and smokes with its own sound; a win floats the won stat over it with a chime.
- Dash recharges in 10 s and Blink in 15 s.

## The hub
- **The Runic Stone** replaces the Credits Stone. It sleeps until you find something in the dungeon, then it starts to speak in runes. Read it often; it has secrets.
- **Credits** moved to the main menu.
- The hub camera is 15 % closer.

## The Blade
- The Blade is locked from the start. Beat the Hollow Crown with the Gun to earn "Sword Unbound" and unlock it. Its forms come later.

## Sound
- Every Gun firing sound varies its pitch by up to 12 %.
