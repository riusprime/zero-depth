# Zero Depth v0.10.5 — Astral Loops
_2026-11-06_ · A whole new Astral look for every screen, power that keeps growing in loops, a Training Room, tougher and fairer bosses, and a smoother game.

## New Features
### Astral look
Every screen now has the same night-sky style: thin silver lines, Cinzel titles and star-lit focus. Cards wear carved frames with a new icon for every card, and face-down cards have carved backs.
- Menus, pause, options, recap, credits and the loading cover.
- The in-run HUD: one continuous health bar with a rune medallion, ability medallions with cooldown arcs, a slim heat bar, the minimap frame, the boss bar and the toasts. HUD text is larger.
- **The danger bar** is a hazard-striped bar that goes from green to red and blinks when full. When the game is pushing back against a strong build, a tag under it reads "The depths answer ×N".
- The shop, card picks, swap panel, Build page, Codex, event panels, hub stations, banners and the build picker.
- Taking a card dissolves it with a silver glare instead of a burn.
- A new set of Astral cursors.
- The dev panel (F10, debug builds only) keeps its old look.

### Loop power
From loop 2 you can get much stronger. Loop 1 plays as before.
- **Past the caps:** crit chance can go past 100 %, and the extra is a chance of a **double crit** (the crit multiplier twice, shown as a larger ice-white number). The crit damage cap rises to ×6 on loop 2 and ×8 from loop 3. Attack speed past its top turns into damage, and the attack-speed card shows how much. Split, Chain Blast and Echo chain one step deeper each loop (up to two more).
- **Starbound cards:** five stackable cards that appear only from loop 2, in the normal card picks, on a gold frame. Each pick adds a stack with no cap, and they work with both weapons.
  - **Arc Jump:** your hits jump to the nearest enemy, weaker each jump.
  - **Rising Edge:** each crit adds crit damage until the room is cleared.
  - **Echo Cast:** a chance that an attack repeats.
  - **Fervour:** each kill adds attack speed until the room is cleared.
  - **Detonation:** an enemy you kill bursts and hurts the ones around it.
- **Constellations:** when a loop starts you pick 1 of 3 for free, on a violet card. A pick is permanent and cannot be rerolled. They stack across loops, and the Build page lists the ones you hold.
  - **Prism:** one more element slot for the Gun (a third element and its pairs turn on); on the Blade, stacked elements weaken less. It can come back once, for a fourth slot.
  - **Twin Star:** your overlevels count twice.
  - **Glass Sky:** double damage, half the max HP.
  - **Hunter's Sky:** +50 % damage to elites and bosses.
  - **Supernova:** every Starbound stack counts twice.
  - **Deep Wounds:** bleed stacks to 20 (was 8), venom to 15 (was 8) and burn to 10 (was 5), and each tick hits 25 % harder.
  - **Starfall:** every 5th crit drops a star on another enemy. Hold Fire at level 3 and it becomes **Meteor**: every 5th attack a meteor falls on the thickest group of enemies near you, and it burns.
- **Overlevels:** from loop 2 every card's level cap rises by 1 a loop, and each overlevel adds damage. The pick shows "+ overlevel".
- The Codex records Starbound cards and Constellations.
- The explosions stay plain: Detonation is a small moon-blue blast, Arc Jump a thin line, and the meteor and the falling star come from the effect packs. No screen shake.

### Training Room
A door at the bottom of the hub leads to a room with an endless dummy and a DPS meter. A terminal spawns enemies you have met, switches your weapon, adds any wand card you have seen at level 1 to 5, and resets the room. Your health never drops below 1 and nothing counts toward your progress.

### Loop rewards and records
- Two new robes: Ouroboros (loop 3) and Abyssal (loop 5).
- Records for your deepest loop and fastest true ending, in the hub and the recap. Achievements for loops 3 and 5.
- Fragments grow with each loop.
- One new rule mutation each loop, from five: Quickened, Lean Mending, Elite Hunt, Shielded, Point Blank.
- Enemy health grows ×3.5 a loop (was ×6), since the rules now carry part of the challenge.

### Enemy duos
Six enemy pairs fight together and are announced as "New: ...": Shield wall, Minefield herd, Pin and pick, Slam and shoot, Crowd the storm, Over the wall.

### Weak to, resists
Enemies are weak to one form or element (x1.25) and resist another (x0.75). The Codex shows "Weak to / Resists".

### New cards
Seven rule cards replace eight flat stat cards: Close Quarters, Third Strike, Reflex, Adrenaline, Vent Mend, Heat Shield and Scavenger. Damage, attack speed, max HP and crit chance stay.

### Wand forecast
The Build page shows a FORECAST under the wand: the spell read left to right and its numbers. The swap panel shows "After the swap".

### A new Codex page
A Lore page with nine entries.

## Bosses and enemies
- **Boss fights last 1 to 2 minutes at your power.** A boss's health is set at its door from how strong you are.
- **The Prism** has a last phase from 50 % health: Facet Lances and the Shattered Kneel.
- **The Brood Mother** can now be your floor 1 boss. Each floor draws from its bosses minus the ones you already met, so a run never meets the same boss twice.
- **The Lantern Shade** and **Clockwork Judge** can be hit more often.
- **The Warlord's** attacks hit 5 % softer.
- **The Hive Lens'** Gaze reaches you faster after it starts turning. **The Foundry's** floor lines and shots never come down together.
- **The Twin Wardens'** violet bolts are now violet fireballs.
- A stolen boss core gives its Legendary card 35 % of the time, and an Epic card otherwise.
- Floor 2 has an Elite Lockdown near the middle of the main path. Floor 3's room before the boss spawns nothing new while you stand in it.
- The director reads your health: when you struggle, packs come earlier, smaller and with longer pauses.
- The 12 old combos that could no longer form and the 5 plain curses (Leaky Core, Price Gouge, Swarm Call, Swift Foes, Withering) are gone. Blood Harvest, Shatter Dash, Slipstream and Spiked Phase stay.

## Combat
- **A snappier Blade:** shorter hit-stop on combo hits, and enemies are pushed back a little when hit (elites half as far, bosses not at all). A dash cancels the end of a Blade swing. The finisher recovers faster.
- Bolts push enemies back only a little.
- A perfect Vent (90 heat or more) refunds your dash.
- Holding the Lance's charge adds no heat. Heat lands when you release.
- **Elements stack with falloff:** an element's damage is reduced when the enemy already carries other elements, for the Gun and the Blade alike. In loop 1 an element's level is capped by the floor (level 1 on floor 1, level 2 on floors 2 and 3, level 3 from floor 4); from loop 2 every element can reach level 3.
- Every stat card now counts exactly, even small fractions.
- **Forms were retuned:** Seekers, Glaive and Laser hit harder, and every form is at least as good as the default. Wave's crowd run is shorter. Split and Mine on the Void are now real gains.
- Some cards are no longer offered for a form: Ring, Backfire, Ricochet, Homing, Echo, Rapid Fire, Chain Blast, Stress Mark and Orbit on the Void, and Ricochet on the Orbs. Ones you already hold still work.
- The Lance charges an element javelin.
- With Homing, Halo's back shots reach far enough to turn and hit the enemies in front.
- The plain Pulse bolt is back. The shock Ring's sparks are smaller and orbit its rim.

## Floors
- About 12 % more chests and altars.
- Kill shards grow for at least 3 minutes a floor, then hold. Shards lie on the floor and are picked up as you come close. Leftovers are collected at the portal.
- Floors 4 and 5 have their own catch-up and boss pace.
- Cracked walls: their runes shine on both sides when you come close, and they now break to the Ring card's blast and to Skill shots. They appear on floors 1 to 3.
- The curse room is easier to see into, and the curse altar shows its details. There is no rune puzzle room.
- Night Rocks and Frozen Shore have calmer walls.
- The shop sells only heal, a reroll and its 4 cards.

## Effects
- **The Laser** is a wider beam with a start flash and a bigger impact. **The Wave's** crest leans away from you, so it reads aimed. **The Void's** Split minis and held Mines are drawn right.
- Hit effects are gone within about 0.2 s of the impact.
- Your ground fire is smaller and dimmer, with at most 8 patches drawn at once. Chain Blast's blasts are sized by their damage.
- Effects from the packs draw at the size the game asks for, and a few that came out too small were sized to their attacks (guard orbs, frost on enemies, frost bursts).
- **The Wisps** are small bright orbs and leave nothing behind.

## Performance
- The world is redrawn once a frame instead of once a tick, and views skip work when nothing has changed.
- **The corner minimap** draws its rooms, doors and icons once each time the map changes, instead of every frame. Before, its drawn pieces piled up with every room you found.
- **Regular enemies are drawn from baked animations** (13 kinds), each kind in one batch, with the same look. Elites, bosses and the Lens Drone are drawn as before.
- Fewer draw calls, cheaper enemy shadows (one simple shadow shape per enemy), particles that cast no shadows, fewer torch shadows, cheaper enemy thinking and path-finding, and effects built at load so they do not hitch the first time.
- Memory from finished floors is freed.
- Your own effects have a budget in busy fights; enemy effects are never cut.
- The low-health red border is drawn once and pulsed, not redrawn every frame.

## Bug Fixes
- The hero no longer stands half inside floor 1's arrival altar.
- Enemies' shadows stand on the floor again: the horde enemies, the Arc Caster and elites had theirs partly or wholly under it.
- Shots, the hero and enemies no longer lag a step behind or flicker on slow frames.
- A Blade swing no longer loses the fraction of damage it carries over to the next hit.
- See-through walls fade smoothly, with no grain.
- The Warden's slam band is easy to see again.
- The minimap arrow points where you walk.
- The hub's walls and floor have no gaps; a dark void with fog replaces the backdrop.
- The secret room opens when it is hit from afar.
- Card names and rarity lines fit inside the frame in every language.
