# Zero Depth v0.10.0 — A New Look for Attacks
_2026-10-31_ · Every attack, hit, element and boss strike has a new look, and a cleanup pass keeps fights readable.

## New Features
### A new look for attacks
Attacks now draw from a licensed set of effect packs, recoloured to each element. Where the packs had nothing, new effects were built in their style.
- **Hits and flashes:** impacts, slash hits, guard sparks, muzzle and cast flashes, crit and bounce flashes, and rune sigils.
- **Your shots:** the orbs take their element's look, and so do Seekers, Wisps, the Lance, Rune Explosion and the other forms.
- **Zones and status:** fire patches, frost and venom fields, the Toxic Fumes cloud, bleed pools, rings, and burn, poison and frost on enemies. Your own fire patches are a cooler colour.
- **Enemy shots:** all 18 enemy shot looks are new, and keep their warm colours so you can tell them from yours.
- **Bosses:** strikes, beams, pillars, fire lanes, the molten floor, shells, blinks, sigils and the final shatter.
- **Lightning** in the same style: strikes, chains, fields, rings, bursts and the shock on enemies.
- **Also new:** bleed drips, the Glaive's ring, the Wave's crest and wash, and the Tidecaller's water.
- **The Blade** leaves a ribbon trail along each swing, with cut sparks, form crescents, eruptions, rings and the Lunge Cleave.
- The Magic Circles credit is in the credits.

### Status stacks you can read
Burn, bleed, poison, shock and frost now show as small icons with an "xN" count in a row over an enemy's health bar. They replace the old shock pips and the poison and bleed dots.

## The Gun
- **Laser with Homing** is one curved beam. It leaves along your aim at once and bends toward its targets, instead of turning your aim. It only bends toward enemies you can see, no more than 90° off your aim, and its curve is smoother.
- **Seekers** search 12 m (was 10.8 m), their darts live 1.3 s (was 1 s), and they only go for enemies you can see.
- **The Void** keeps its v0.9.5 look.

## Cleaner fights
- A kill shows the old lit chunks, with no smoke or flash, and fire explosions leave no smoke behind.
- Effects are drawn at their right size: no more large white starbursts or oversized flashes.
- Enemy lob and rain shots keep their own effects: a landed shot's trail no longer jumps to the next one and streaks across the screen.
- **The Foundry:** its fire no longer draws twice on the molten floor.
- **Blink** flashes in 0.28 s (was 1.1 s at each end).
- Frost and splat marks, bleed splats and the puddles after venom and bleed bursts no longer carry extra effects on top.

## Performance
- Effects warm up once a session at the main menu, so they don't stall the first time they appear and floors load faster.
- Each effect's own light is switched off; effects are pooled and reused, with a cap per kind that follows your Effects density setting.
