# Zero Depth v0.11.0 — Fights Worth Learning
_2026-11-08_ · Bosses you beat by learning them, a Laser that fires lances, elemental spells, horde surges, new enemies for every biome and rooms that have a purpose.

## New Features
### Bosses
Three bosses now have a mechanic you can learn and use, instead of waiting out a shield.
- **The Gatekeeper** raises three quake pillars from phase 2. They pulse a little damage every 3 s. Lure its Shock Lanes cross through a pillar: the pillar shatters and the Gatekeeper is stunned for 2 s and takes 50 % more damage, at most twice a phase. The bar reads "STUNNED · VULNERABLE".
- **The Lantern Shade** is never immune. It takes 40 % damage in the dark and 150 % inside a lit lantern's pool. Step on a lantern to light it for 6 s. The dark lasts 9 s and the light 4 s. The bar and the damage numbers show which you are getting.
- **The Twin Wardens** fight in three phases, each a full health bar. Phase 1: the yellow twin (Shield Bash, Chain Sweep, Charge, Ground Slam). Phase 2: the purple twin (Crossfire, Orb Volley, Rune Ring, Blink). Phase 3: both together, and the twin you can hurt swaps every 10 s. The resting twin takes no damage and shows a small spark when hit.
- **Hints:** the first two times you meet one of these mechanics, an arrow and a ping point at what matters. Options → Accessibility → Boss hints turns them off.
- **Animation:** every boss moves less stiffly: a small dip before each wind-up, a recoil after a strike, a wider idle sway. Timings and telegraphs are the same.

### The Laser
The Laser is now a lance. While you hold fire it shoots an instant lance every 3 s that goes through every enemy in its line, each one after the first taking 15 % less (never under 40 %). One lance applies three hits' worth of status. You keep full speed while holding it.
- With **Homing** the lance bends in one smooth curve through up to 8 enemies, then runs on to the first wall. With **Twin** and Homing each lance takes its own enemies, bowing out and curving in (on one enemy, they meet on it).
- **Rapid Fire** shortens the wait and lowers the damage, **Echo** strikes the same path again at half damage, **Big Shot** makes a wider, stronger lance. Attack speed shortens the wait at half value.
- The Laser always pierces, so the Pierce and Mine cards are no longer offered with it.

### Elemental spells
Holding an element card (Fire, Frost, Lightning, Venom, Bleed) adds that element's three spells to your card picks. The element of your Wardrobe rune makes its spells show up more often.
- You have 4 spell slots, apart from the wand. Each spell casts by itself on its own cooldown (about 12 s) and aims at the thickest group of enemies near you. When the slots are full, a new spell replaces your lowest one.
- Taking a spell you hold levels it up (Lv 1–3, Lv 4–5 from loop 2), and every level changes something you can see.
- 15 spells: Blizzard, Frost Nova, Ice Lances; Fireball, Burning Ground, Meteor; Sky Strike, Ball Lightning, Static Field; Venom Orb, Toxic Cloud, Spitter Vine; Blood Thorns, Hemorrhage, Leech (the only one that heals).
- Four squares above the ability bar show each spell's cooldown and flash when it casts.

### Horde surges
Once or twice a floor, "A horde is coming!": 30 to 60 small critters pour in over 10 s. They have little health and only hurt on contact, so area attacks and spells shine. Surges never start in an Overrun room, an arena, a Lockdown or a boss room.
- Each biome has its own critter: Dust Mite (Ruins), Cave Bat (Night Rocks, flies), Ember Flea (Red Canyon, hops), Ice Crab (Frozen Shore, slides), Star Mote (Starfall, floats).

### New enemies and biome rosters
Each biome now has its own enemies: a shared core (Charger, Needle, Swarmer, Warden), two enemies of its own and one local variant. Deeper floors still add the later kinds.
- **Ruins:** Wire Weaver (strings live cables across the room), Scrap Hound (darts in from behind while you attack something else), Rubble Warden (its crust falls off as cover).
- **Night Rocks:** Rock Lurker (waits as a boulder; its cracks glow), Stone Thrower (throws rocks that stay as cover), Dim Needle (its shots stay dark until 4 m from you).
- **Red Canyon:** Kiln Beetle (bursts when it dies), Cinder Priest (makes lava vents erupt; kill it first), Ember Charger (leaves a fire trail).
- **Frozen Shore:** Frost Spitter (leaves slick ice you slide on), Glacier Turtle (its ice shell blocks shots from the front), Frost Charger (leaves an ice slick).
- **Starfall:** Star Warper (blinks onto a star mark beside you; stand on the mark to stun it), Gravity Node (pulls every shot near it, yours and theirs), Split Sniper (its shot splits in three).
- Every new enemy is weak to one attack form or element and resists another, spread so no form is the answer to everything.

### Rooms with a purpose
Most large rooms are now built around something: a **camp**, a **stone-ring hall** of leaning standing stones, or a **quarry**. Each has its own layout with some variety every run, and doorways and item spots stay clear.

### The Runic Stone
The Runic Stone's lines now tell one short story, and they lead somewhere.

## Bug Fixes
- **Void:** with Twin and Split, voids no longer jump or vanish mid-flight. At the void limit the extra small voids are dropped quietly, and Mine stops each Twin void on its own lane.
- **The Lantern Shade's wisps** are hidden in the dark again (they show inside a light or within 4 m), and its dense wisp patterns leave wider gaps to dodge through.
- **The Siege Engine's** second volley stays hidden until the first lands, then shows for 1.2 s. Its blasts no longer leave smoke over the next telegraph.
- **Pierce:** a held card your current attack form doesn't use now says "Does nothing with this form" on the wand screen.
- The Lens Drone is drawn from baked animations like the other regular enemies.
