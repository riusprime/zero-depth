# Zero Depth v0.9.0 — World and Weapons
_2026-10-24_ · Five floors, four new bosses, ten Gun forms, a hub to come home to, and floors full of secrets.

## New Features
### Five floors
A run is now five floors, small at first and longer later (about 10 to 19 rooms), with every biome once. Floors 4
and 5 have four new bosses:
- **The Tidecaller** pulls and pushes the whole room.
- **The Twin Wardens** are two bodies with one health bar and a chain between them.
- **The Clockwork Judge** replays your own path as strikes.
- **The Lantern Shade** puts the lights out for a while; relight the lanterns.

Each floor you clear gives you +15 max HP, and the heal between floors is 60 %.

### The Rune Ring
The main menu is now Play, Continue, Options and Quit. Everything else lives in the hub, the Rune Ring: the Portal
Machine (pick your weapon and walk up into the portal), the Rune Archive (the Codex and your collectibles), the
Wardrobe, the Progression Station, the Trophy Area and the Credits Stone. After a run you see a summary, then Restart
with one press or go back to the hub.

### Rune Fragments
Runs earn Rune Fragments: bosses, floors cleared, secrets found, the purple Deep portal, the final boss and
collectibles. Spend them at the Progression Station on robes.
- Four robes cost 75, 100, 125 and 150; two epic robes cost 250 each.
- **The Fallen's Regalia** shows as ??? until bought: the Fallen's dark cloak and crown, red-violet eyes and a violet
  attack. **Divine Hollow Crown** is its radiant twin with a halo.
- **Affinities:** pick Ember, Storm, Venom, Astral or Guardian at the Wardrobe before a run. Each has a gain and a
  cost; its rune shows on your back and under your feet.

### The arrival and the final approach
- Floor 1 opens on a big stone altar that wakes up rune by rune before you pop in.
- Floor portals are tall swirling ovals that grow after the boss falls and collapse once you go through; you step
  out of the same portal on the next floor.
- The Ascension portal now leads up a quiet stair causeway to a purple altar. Wake its two pylons and the Colossus
  rises.
- The arrival, the boss door and the final travel vibrate instead of shaking the camera, and the boss door's boom
  lands with the slab.

## Items
### Ten Gun forms
Your Gun's shot now has a **Form**: Orbs, Laser, Bomb, Void, Lance, Scatter, Glaive, Seekers, Wave or Wisps.
- The Lance charges a swirling circle and fires a ball that grows; the Laser is a held beam; the Bomb is lobbed.
- Void fires slow black holes that tug the crowd. It unlocks the first time you travel through the purple Deep
  portal.
- Charge, Boomerang and Explosive are retired.

### Wand slots
- Each wand holds one Form, one Pattern (Twin, Halo, Ring or Backfire), two Secondaries (Spiral and Swirl among them)
  and two Elements.
- A new Form or Pattern card says what it replaces, and the old one burns. A full Secondary or Element slot opens the
  Swap panel.
- **Twin and Spread are one card:** two shots side by side, three fanned at level 5.
- Legendary Power and Haste are weaker (+32.5 % and +23.4 %).
- Runs saved in v0.8.0 still load; an over-full wand is trimmed.

## World
- **Lockdowns:** 1–2 rooms a floor where bars seal the doors for a short fight. The exits always open within 90 s.
- **Secrets:** timed doors with two switches and 15 s of beeps, rune pillars to turn until they match a carving,
  cracked walls only your Skill breaks, and hidden rooms you can't see until they open. Secrets start on floor 2.
- **The purple Deep portal is earned:** find the purple-rune room on floor 2 or 3 and that floor's boss opens it.
- **Shrines:** Fortune (the old gamble shrine), Blood (pay HP for a card) and Chaos (fight for a card).
- **Healing fountains,** containers and explosive barrels, each in its biome's look.
- **16 collectibles** to find, each with a short line in the Rune Archive.

## Enemies
- **Elites:** the Breaker, the Rift Hunter and the Conduit, on floors 4 and 5, with a name over their bar and a 30 %
  chance to drop a core. The old double-HP elites are gone.
- Elites hurt you only with attacks they wind up first, never on touch.
- Enemies never hit you through a shut door or a wall.
- Closed fights are never in the smallest rooms, and their crowd fits the room.

## Combat
- Enemies no longer explode when they die: Fire's kills leave a burning patch.
- At most 12 projectiles an attack; extras merge into bigger shots.
- New launch, travel and impact looks for every form and element.
