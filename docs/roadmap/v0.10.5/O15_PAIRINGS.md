# O15: enemy pairings (theory audit P14; the lead's overnight pick, owner reviews)

Adams and Dormans (emergence) and Koster (new patterns from known parts): authored duos make new problems from the
enemies we already have. A **pairing** is a rule in the spawner: when a pack of the **lead** kind arrives on a floor
where the **partner** kind is already unlocked (GAME_BLUEPRINT §E, "Enemies by floor"), then with the duo's chance
1–2 partners join the pack, standing where the duo's role puts them. Each pairing is a data row; every number is a
starting value.

| Duo | Lead → partner | From floor | Chance | Partners | Where they stand | The problem it poses |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shield wall | Shield Bearer → Mender | 3 | 30 % | 1 | behind the bearers (away from the hero) | Kill the healer behind the wall: flank, or break the shield first |
| Minefield herd | Charger → Mine Layer | 2 | 25 % | 1 | behind the chargers | The chargers' lanes push you over fresh mines: dash across, not along |
| Pin and pick | Swarmer pack → Sniper | 3 | 25 % | 1 | far side of the room, at range | The swarm pins you in place; the sniper punishes standing still |
| Slam and shoot | Warden → Needle | 1 | 25 % | 2 | flanking the warden, at range | Dodge the slam circle into the needles' lines, or back off and eat bolts |
| Crowd the storm | Arc Caster → Splitter | 1 | 20 % | 1 | in front of the caster | Splitting it in the arc field multiplies your problem: pull it out first |
| Over the wall | Shield Bearer → Bomb Drone | 2 | 25 % | 1 | above and behind the bearers | Bombs lob over a wall you can't shoot through: go round |

Rules: a pairing never pushes the room past its alive cap (the partners count toward it, and are dropped first);
never in boss rooms, arenas' fixed waves or Lockdowns' formations; announced the first time each duo appears in a run,
like new kinds ("New: Shield wall"). Drawn on the spawner's existing stream.
