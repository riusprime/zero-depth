# Zero Depth v0.10.5 — Astral Loops
_Release date: —_ · A whole new Astral look for every screen, power that keeps growing in loops, a Training Room, tougher and fairer bosses, and a smoother game.

## New Features
### Astral look
Every screen now has the same night-sky style: thin silver lines, Cinzel titles and star-lit focus. Cards wear carved frames with a new icon for every card, and face-down cards have carved backs.
- Menus, pause, options, recap, credits and the loading cover.
- The in-run HUD: one continuous health bar with a rune medallion, ability medallions with cooldown arcs, a slim danger bar and heat bar, the minimap frame, the boss bar and the toasts.
- The shop, card picks, swap panel, Build page, Codex, event panels and hub stations.
- A new set of Astral cursors.
- The dev panel (F10, debug builds only) keeps its old look.

### Loop power
From loop 2 you can get much stronger. Loop 1 plays as before.
- **Starbound cards:** five stackable cards that appear only from loop 2, in the normal card picks, on a gold frame. Each pick adds a stack with no cap, and they work with both weapons.
  - **Arc Jump:** your hits jump to the nearest enemy, weaker each jump.
  - **Rising Edge:** each crit adds crit damage until the room is cleared.
  - **Echo Cast:** a chance that an attack repeats.
  - **Fervour:** each kill adds attack speed until the room is cleared.
  - **Detonation:** an enemy you kill bursts and hurts the ones around it.
- **Constellations:** when a loop starts you pick 1 of 3 for free, on a violet card. A pick is permanent and cannot be rerolled. They stack across loops, and the Build page lists the ones you hold.
  - **Prism:** one more element slot for the Gun (a third element and its pairs turn on); the Blade's element falloff softens. It can come back once, for a fourth slot.
  - **Twin Star:** your overlevels count twice.
  - **Glass Sky:** double damage, half the max HP.
  - **Hunter's Sky:** elites and bosses take more damage from you.
  - **Supernova:** every Starbound stack counts twice.
  - **Deep Wounds:** bleed, venom and burn stack much higher and each stack hits harder.
  - **Starfall:** every 5th crit drops a star on another enemy. Hold Fire at level 3 and it becomes **Meteor**: every 5th attack a meteor falls on a group of enemies.
- **Overlevels:** from loop 2 every card's level cap rises by 1 a loop, and each overlevel adds damage. The pick shows "+ overlevel".
- The Codex records Starbound cards and Constellations.
- Explosions stay plain: small moon-blue blasts and thin lines, no screen shake.

### Training Room
A door at the bottom of the hub leads to a room with an endless dummy and a DPS meter. A terminal spawns enemies you have met, adds any wand card you have seen at level 1 to 5, and resets. Your health never drops below 1 and nothing counts toward your progress.

### Loop rewards and records
- Two new robes: Ouroboros (loop 3) and Abyssal (loop 5).
- Records for your deepest loop and fastest true ending, in the hub and the recap. Achievements for loops 3 and 5.
- Fragments grow with each loop.
- One new rule mutation each loop, from five: Quickened, Lean Mending, Elite Hunt, Shielded, Point Blank.

### Enemy duos
Six enemy pairs fight together and are announced as "New: ...": Shield wall, Minefield herd, Pin and pick, Slam and shoot, Crowd the storm, Over the wall.

### Weak to, resists
Enemies are weak to one form or element (x1.25) and resist another (x0.75). The Codex shows "Weak to / Resists".

### New cards
Seven rule cards replace eight flat stat cards: Close Quarters, Third Strike, Reflex, Adrenaline, Vent Mend, Heat Shield and Scavenger. Damage, attack speed, max HP and crit chance stay.

### Wand forecast
The Build page shows a FORECAST under the wand: the spell read left to right and its numbers. The swap panel shows "After the swap".

### A new Codex page
A Lore page with nine entries.

## Bosses and enemies
- **Boss fights last 1 to 2 minutes at your power.** A boss's health is set at its door from how strong you are.
- **The Prism** has a last phase from 50 % health: Facet Lances and the Shattered Kneel.
- **The Brood Mother** can now be your floor 1 boss. Each floor draws from its bosses minus the ones you already met, so a run never meets the same boss twice.
- **The Lantern Shade** and **Clockwork Judge** can be hit more often.
- **The Warlord's** attacks hit 5 % softer.
- **The Hive Lens'** Gaze reaches you faster after it starts turning. **The Foundry's** floor lines and shots never come down together.
- A boss core is Legendary more often.
- Floor 2 has an Elite Lockdown near the middle of the main path. Floor 3's room before the boss spawns nothing new while you stand in it.
- The director reads your health: when you struggle, packs come earlier, smaller and with longer pauses.

## Combat
- **A snappier Blade:** longer hit-stop on combo hits, and enemies are pushed back a little when hit (bosses are not). A dash cancels the end of a Blade swing. The finisher recovers faster.
- A perfect Vent (90 heat or more) refunds your dash.
- Holding the Lance's charge adds no heat. Heat lands when you release.
- Elements stack with falloff: an element's damage is reduced when the enemy already carries other elements. Element damage and element level caps follow the floor, and open up from loop 2.
- Every stat card now counts exactly, even small fractions.
- Forms were retuned: Seekers, Glaive and Laser hit harder, and every form is at least as good as the default. Wave's crowd run is shorter.
- The Lance charges an element javelin.
- Bolts push enemies back half as far.

## Floors
- About 12 % more chests and altars.
- Kill shards grow for at least 3 minutes a floor, then hold. Shards lie on the floor and are picked up as you come close. Leftovers are collected at the portal.
- Floors 4 and 5 have their own catch-up and boss pace.
- Cracked walls are easier to see and now break to the Ring card's blast and to Skill shots. They appear on floors 1 to 3.
- The curse room is easier to see into. There is no rune puzzle room.
- Night Rocks and Frozen Shore have calmer walls.
- The shop sells only heal, a reroll and its 4 cards.

## Performance
- The world is redrawn once a frame instead of once a tick.
- Fewer draw calls, cheaper enemy shadows, cheaper enemy thinking and path-finding, and effects built at load so they do not hitch the first time.
- Memory from finished floors is freed.
- Your own effects have a budget in busy fights; enemy effects are never cut.

## Bug Fixes
- The hero no longer stands half inside floor 1's arrival altar.
- See-through walls fade smoothly, with no grain.
- The Warden's slam band is easy to see again.
- The minimap arrow points where you walk.
- Hit and fire effects on the hero leave sooner.
- The hub's walls and floor have no gaps; a dark void with fog replaces the backdrop.
- The secret room opens when it is hit from afar.
- Card names and rarity lines fit inside the frame in every language.
