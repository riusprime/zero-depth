# O18: one lore line per system (theory audit P18; overnight lead pick, owner reviews)

Schell's tetrad: a line of fiction per system makes its mechanic mean something. One entry per system on a new
**Lore** page of the Codex, unlocked from the start (no spoilers beyond what a first run shows, except the last two,
which unlock when their system is first met). Written by the lead; the owner keeps, edits or cuts each line.

| Key | Unlocks | English | Español |
|---|---|---|---|
| `LORE_DEPTHS` | always | The Depths were a city once. Each floor down is older, and it remembers less of what it was. | Las Profundidades fueron una ciudad. Cada piso más abajo es más antiguo y recuerda menos de lo que fue. |
| `LORE_HEAT` | always | Your core drinks the fight. Run it hot and it sings; let it boil and it bites back. Vent before it does. | Tu núcleo bebe del combate. Si arde, canta; si hierve, muerde. Libéralo antes de que lo haga. |
| `LORE_WAND` | always | A spell is a sentence. Each card you set is a word, and the order is the grammar. | Un hechizo es una frase. Cada carta que colocas es una palabra, y el orden es la gramática. |
| `LORE_SHARDS` | always | Shards are what the Depths shed when something dies in them. Everyone down here trades in endings. | Los fragmentos son lo que las Profundidades sueltan cuando algo muere en ellas. Aquí abajo todos comercian con finales. |
| `LORE_CURSES` | first curse taken | A curse is a bargain the Depths keep for you. The price never changes; only how much you can afford. | Una maldición es un trato que las Profundidades guardan por ti. El precio nunca cambia; solo lo que puedes pagar. |
| `LORE_DEEP` | first Deep portal seen | The violet gate leads where the light gives up. The rewards are real. So is everything else. | La puerta violeta lleva adonde la luz se rinde. Las recompensas son reales. Todo lo demás también. |
| `LORE_RUNES` | first rune fragment | The runes are the city's last memories, carved where it could not forget them. Gather them and it remembers you. | Las runas son los últimos recuerdos de la ciudad, grabados donde no podía olvidarlos. Reúnelas y te recordará. |
| `LORE_CROWN` | first time the final boss is reached | The Hollow Crown went down first, to rule what lay beneath. It found nothing to rule, and kept descending. | La Corona Hueca bajó la primera, para reinar sobre lo de abajo. No encontró nada que gobernar, y siguió descendiendo. |
| `LORE_ASCENSION` | first Ascension portal | Up is not out. The Depths turn over, and the top becomes the bottom again, a little hungrier. | Subir no es salir. Las Profundidades se dan la vuelta, y lo de arriba vuelve a ser el fondo, con un poco más de hambre. |

Titles: `LORE_<X>_TITLE` — The Depths / Las Profundidades; Overclock / Sobrecarga; The Wand / La Varita; Shards /
Fragmentos; Curses / Maldiciones; The Deep / Lo Profundo; Runes / Runas; The Hollow Crown / La Corona Hueca;
Ascension / Ascensión. A locked entry shows "???" like the Codex's other locked entries.
