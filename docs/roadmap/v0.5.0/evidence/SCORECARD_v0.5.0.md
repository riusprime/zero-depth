## Interpretation
- **The exit gate's mechanism holds:** every one of the 23 cells is filled by one command, in about 34 minutes on
  this machine, and the run records are byte-reproducible. Quick mode (`-- --quick`) and the unit test
  (`tests/unit/scorecard/`) check that every cell is produced and well-formed.
- **On this base the bots die on floor 1** (every normal policy, 360 of 360 runs; competent at Room 6–8 of floor 1 most
  often). That matches the owner's v0.4.0 playtest ("extremely hard from the beginning") and is what TU is changing; it
  also empties or flattens most cells: M-FLOOR and M-RUN have no data, M-GAP, M-GENERALIST, M-THREAT and M-DEAD compare
  zeros, M-HAZARD's "met" only says every biome killed every run. These cells must be rerun after TU.
- **Misses that do not depend on difficulty:** M-ENGINE (0.667 vs ≥ 0.9) and M-DROUGHT (median 4 vs ≤ 2) measure the
  reward schedule in the rooms the bots walked; M-STRESS (the stress tags still name only v0.2.0's bleed and guard);
  M-LOOP (buying a level-up of a free ability and salvaging it pays 50 for 30: the refund counts every level, not the
  levels bought); M-LIMIT (Vampiric Core's cap clip emits LIMIT, which the band counts); M-BENCH (the stress scenes,
  known misses since v0.3.0 and v0.4.0 SC; this container was shared with other suites while it ran, so the timings are
  an upper bound, not the reference machine).
- **What the numbers don't prove:** bots are not players (SCORECARD §1). Pick rates measure ScoreBot's score table.
  Presets are SCORECARD §3's starting values, uncalibrated. `exploit:chain` starts with every combo's items, a
  test-side write; its wins (Gun 6/20, Blade 2/20, won runs 5.8–7.4 min) say a full kit carries a bot through, not
  what a run is.
- **For the owner** (nothing was retuned here; SCORECARD §1.3): M-LOOP's salvage refund, M-LIMIT's cap-clip LIMIT
  (count it or not), M-STRESS's tags for the current archetypes, and the schedule misses M-ENGINE / M-DROUGHT are
  decisions; the difficulty-bound cells wait for TU.
