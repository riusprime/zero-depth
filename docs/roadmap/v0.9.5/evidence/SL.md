# v0.9.5 Step SL: the Blade is locked until the Hollow Crown falls to the Gun

Machine: owner's PC (i7-10700K, RTX 3060), Godot 4.7.2, `--language en`, each run with its own `APPDATA`.
Base: `claude/v095-feedback` merged (includes 28218c03).

## What changed

- `SwordLock` (`src/application/sword_lock.gd`): the Blade is unlocked exactly when the profile holds the
  `sword_unbound` achievement (profile `achievements.unlocked`), so the unlock is saved and loaded with the profile
  and needs no new field or profile version. `grant()` is the test/dev path.
- Achievement `sword_unbound` (`data/achievements/sword_unbound.tres`, group bosses, stat `final:won_gun`, validated
  by the existing content tests). `AchievementTracker` reports the new stat `final:won_gun` when the final arena is won
  and the wand's weapon is the Gun (`final:won` still counts every win). Added to `AchievementDefinition.STATS`.
- `Wardrobe.build_of` (used by the new run, the hub's hero and the picker's focus) falls back to the Gun while the Blade
  is locked, so a fresh or remembered-Blade profile defaults to the Gun.
- `BuildPicker` / `BuildCard`: the locked Blade draws a padlock over its symbol and the condition line in place of its
  damage lines; a pick (Enter, click, pad A) does nothing but flash the padlock red. The Playground's picker (dev tool)
  passes no locks, so the Blade stays usable there. The sim, bots and balance labs never go through the picker.
- Strings (appended to `locale/strings.csv`, `.translation` files regenerated by the import):
  `ACH_SWORD_UNBOUND_NAME/DESC`, `BUILD_BLADE_LOCKED`.
- A run saved mid-run with the Blade loads and finishes (the run's `build_id` is the save's; the lock is read only by
  the picker and the default pick).
- Test helper: `E2e.boot()` now grants the unlock by default (`SwordLock.grant`), `e.blade_locked = true` before
  `boot()` keeps it locked. test_e2e_achievements sets it (it counts a fresh profile's achievements).
  test_achievements' size bound raised from 45 to 50 (the book has one more achievement).

## Retroactive grant

None. No stored stat records "the Hollow Crown beaten with the Gun" (`final:won` and `runs:won_gun` exist, but not
together per run), and the owner decision is that the Blade is locked for every profile, the owner's included. A
profile that already holds `sword_unbound` (impossible before this step) reads as unlocked.

## Deviations

- Spanish text follows the order's wording ("Vence a la Corona Hueca con la Pistola"); the existing `es` boss strings
  still say "Hollow Crown" (ACH_FINAL_BOSS_DESC), so the two differ until a wording pass.
- No deny sound: the game has no menu deny cue (the Void's lock is a card-offer lock, not a picker). The padlock flashes
  red instead.
- A padlocked card's Enter/click is handled in the picker (no `picked` signal), not by disabling the Button, so focus
  and hover still work on it.
