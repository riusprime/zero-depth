## Get the build
- Branch `claude/lucid-fermat-9wv2tf` (v0.7.5 + v0.8.0; not in `main` yet):
  `git fetch origin && git switch claude/lucid-fermat-9wv2tf && git pull`, open in Godot 4.7.2, let it import, Play.
- Fastest way to the bosses: Main menu → **F10** → **Playground** (top bar) → **F1** (spawn any boss, the final boss and "jump to phase",
  god mode, any card at any level, the DPS meter). F10 → Bosses for every new number.

## What's new
- **Enemies:** one colour family per role (palette A); melee, the Sniper and the Needle wind up with their body (a
  crouch and a flashing eye), no red ground marks; only the Arc Caster's rune and the Mine Layer's mines mark the ground.
- **Bosses:** keyframed animations; every attack strikes in 3D (stone spikes, spears, acid, a beam, shells, molten
  metal); one signature each: Gatekeeper pillars, Warlord front shield + recalled javelins, Brood Mother eggs to pop,
  Hive Lens sweeping gaze, Siege Engine ricochets, Foundry lava and a shrinking arena.
- **The final boss** (End the run, any loop): the cosmic arena; the Colossus, the Prism, the Nemesis; the true ending.
- **Builds:** Blade combinations (B1–B7, two new Blade cards); the Gun's split/explode stacking tamed; a frame budget for
  big builds.
- **Loops:** HP ×6, damage ×1.2 per loop (×100 cap).
- **Rooms:** 24 designed layouts; the shop is your desert stall in a designed shop room per biome.
- **Menus and cards:** Ember stone; the shard cards are gone.
