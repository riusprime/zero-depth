ZeroDepth · Boss Playtest Feedback

18 November 2026  |  Twin Wardens, Fallen, Foundry, Tidecaller

1. Twin Wardens

Overall: The fight feels great, with the purple phase currently stronger than the amber phase.

Phase 1 — Amber / physical twin

The ranged attacks can be avoided simply by standing outside their reach; the boss is not forcing meaningful engagement.

The physical twin lacks convincing melee pressure. Desired behavior: pursue the player, stay in melee range, and use a couple of close-range attacks that require moving out; ranged attacks become relevant when the player retreats.

The fast, explosive charge triggers only when the player is nearby. This conflicts with the incentive to stay far away to avoid the ranged attacks.

The charge should occur more often, with an approximately 10–15-second cooldown.

In this phase, the charge is too easily avoided by running. Feedback: remove the visible aiming line / long tell, make it near-immediate and anticipate player movement so that dodging with the player dash is necessary.

Smoke appears at the boss’s feet on every attack and does not look good.

Phase 2 — Purple / magic twin

The reflect shield feels great. Overall, this phase feels better than phase 1.

The ground-marked zone followed by an arc of projectiles is too easy to bypass by simply stepping outside the marked area.

There is little incentive to remain inside the marked zone; staying outside is generally easier. The projectile arc weakens the intended mechanic.

The remaining mechanics feel fine.

Phase 3 — Both twins

Amber still relies too much on ranged attacks; the physical/melee identity remains weak.

The charge used to break Purple’s shield is infrequent. An additional ~1 second of charge/aiming time was requested, although aiming felt manageable after further attempts.

The near-instant, dash-required charge feedback applies to phase 1 only; in phase 3, the player needs enough time to aim the charge at Purple’s shield.

Purple starts the phase with the shield already active. Feedback: it should activate as a mechanic during the fight instead of being present immediately.

The player can focus Amber and ignore Purple’s shield. There is currently no strong reason to engage with or complete the shield-breaking mechanic.

2. Fallen

Early impression: the shield sequence feels too similar to the previous shielding phase and is relatively easy to dodge.

The clockwork sequence felt great, and the fight improved noticeably once the broader mechanics became apparent.

Overall impression is very positive: the Fallen fight feels great. Full evaluation in a normal gameplay run is still pending.

3. Foundry

Projectiles and attack patterns

Fireballs still appear to use previous/legacy VFX. The effects are inconsistent and buggy, sometimes appearing yellow and sometimes white.

Several projectile patterns have gaps too narrow for the player to pass through reliably. This is especially noticeable in the attack emitting fire from the boss’s head and another dense pattern.

Fire firing in both directions at once can feel practically impossible to dodge.

Boss visuals

The new shiny-looking material/texture is less appealing than the previous appearance.

Elemental effects, especially fire, can cover the boss in orange and obscure the model, significantly hurting its visual identity.

4. Tidecaller

The slower pacing introduced in the rework feels better. The fight previously had too much happening at once.

Dancing water mechanic

During the pattern with three water-covered zones and one safe zone, the safe zone is displayed in yellow. It should appear as empty map instead of yellow.

The water has a noticeable flickering/twitching visual bug during this mechanic, which is distracting.
