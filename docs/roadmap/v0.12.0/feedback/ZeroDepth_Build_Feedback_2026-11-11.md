# ZeroDepth — Latest Build Feedback

Pasted by the owner in chat on 2026-11-18 (verbatim).

**Session:** November 11, 2026
**Scope:** Performance, graphics, enemy behavior, visual effects, UI, and boss mechanics.

## 1. Performance & FPS

**General performance**

- Initial performance looks good, with approximately **120 FPS without enemies** and **80 FPS while shooting** at close to 2K resolution.
- However, performance drops significantly during certain combat situations, even when there are relatively few enemies on screen.

**Specific performance observations**

| Scenario            | FPS   | Notes                                                            |
| ------------------- | ----- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| No enemies          | \~120 | Close to 2K resolution                                           |
| Shooting            | \~80  | Close to 2K resolution                                           |
| Seekers + Venom     | \~40  | Not many enemies on screen; unclear whether Venom is responsible |
| Starfall combat     | \~35  | Approximately 30–40 enemies                                      |
| Frozen Shore combat | \~60  | New enemies and animations, at 1600 × 900                        |

**Concerns**

- The FPS reduction when using Seekers and Venom seems disproportionately large.
- Starfall suffers significant performance degradation during combat.
- Frozen Shore reaches only around 60 FPS at 1600 × 900, despite the relatively limited amount of action visible on screen.
- Overall performance during combat does not seem consistent with the complexity of what is being rendered.

## 2. Graphics & Display Settings

**Resolution options**

- The options menu currently lacks resolution selection.
- Players should be able to choose a resolution different from their monitor's native resolution.
- This functionality has not yet been implemented.

**Advanced graphics customization**

- Explore support for GPU-specific rendering and optimization technologies, including DLSS.
- Expand graphics options beyond simple High/Low quality presets.
- Explore individual settings for shadows, lighting effects, texture filtering, and other relevant rendering parameters.
- The objective is to allow players to adapt graphics settings to their hardware with more granular control.

## 3. Biome Visuals & Enemy Variations

**Starfall — Ground marks (review similar effects in other biomes just in case)**

- Ground marks visibly display the rectangular or square boundaries of their textures.
- They should appear integrated into the ground without visible texture borders.

**Enemy variations by biome**

- Explore distinct textures or color variations for enemies across different biomes.
- For example, Chargers in Red Canyon and Frozen Shore should have visually distinguishable appearances.
- Basic enemies and their spellcasting variants should be recognizable at a glance.
- Currently, the difference between the standard enemy and its variation is not sufficiently clear.

## 4. Enemy Behavior & Visual Effects

**New Void Orb enemy**

- The new floating orb with a void center and surrounding circular structure currently has no noticeable gameplay behavior.
- Its only interaction appears to be requiring the player to kill it.
- It should have an identifiable gameplay function or mechanic.
- One possibility mentioned during testing was a slight pulling effect.

**Enemy smoke**

- Many enemies still produce smoke effects that remain visible for too long.
- The amount of lingering smoke is excessive.
- Remove the smoke effects associated with these enemies.

**Duplicate explosions**

- Review the explosion effects of all enemies, particularly the newly introduced ones.
- Some may be triggering or displaying explosions twice.
- Ensure death effects are not duplicated.

## 5. Boss Feedback — Gatekeeper

**Earthquake spike attack**

- The spikes generated by the Gatekeeper's earthquake attack should appear closer to the boss.
- The spikes should emerge sequentially, rather than all at once.
- Their animation should progress outward, from the closest spike to the farthest, creating a wave-like pattern.
- The intended behavior is similar to the spike sequence used in the first phase of the final boss.

## 6. UI & Interaction Bugs

**Map / Minimap**

- Pressing Select opens the map.
- However, the minimap is not displaying as expected.
- The map functionality and minimap visibility need to be reviewed.

**Blood Price — Old Pulse Gun**

- A Blood Price interaction still displays the old Pulse Gun.
- The weapon cannot be picked up.
- This appears to be a remaining issue related to the old weapon implementation.
