# v0.6.1: playtest build (owner)

The shard look on top of v0.6.0, plus the VFX pass from `claude/keen-volta-ht74pz` (real fire, explosions and
lightning; frost, venom, void and bleed on every attack form; enemy status looks). Answers below are **OWNER ONLY**.

## Get the build
1. When the PR into `main` is merged, the **Windows** workflow runs on `main`:
   <https://github.com/riusprime/zero-depth/actions/workflows/windows.yml>. Pick the newest green run on `main`.
2. Download `game_windows_x86_64`, unzip `game_windows_x86_64.zip`, run `game.exe`.

## What's new
- **Build picker** in your art: the title plaque ("Choose your weapon"), the Blade and Gun frames and emblems.
- **Your plaques** on pop-ups, combo badges, event choices, shop rows, the shrine result and banners.
- **Shard world pieces:** altars (blue, Deep violet, legendary gold), heal orbs, shard gems, dropped cores;
  crystals round each portal in its colour.
- **Room crystals:** about a fifth of each room's stone wall base, mostly corners, only on stone; 1–2 crystal rooms
  per floor with big crystals.
- **VFX:** fire, bomb explosions and lightning from your textures; frost, venom, void and bleed looks; enemy
  statuses on the body.

## Known, measured here (not felt)
- Screenshots came from software rendering in a cloud container; the look and the frame rate on your PC are not
  known. Room crystals add about 0.16 s to a floor's build on the cloud machine (once, at load).
- The other biome tints of the crystals were not screenshotted.
