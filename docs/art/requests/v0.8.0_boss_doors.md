# Art request: one boss door per biome (v0.8.0, owner O82)

Owner, 2026-10-18: "Next missing 3D models are doors for each biome, we should have 5 doors that match each biome for
the boss room … they should be similar but with small different details and colors based on the biome".

Today the boss door is code-made (`src/presentation/world_view/boss_door_view.gd`): a stone slab between two jambs
under a lintel, with a glowing red seal on both faces. The slab **sinks into the floor** as you walk up, **rises and
shuts** behind you, and its seal **goes dark** when the boss dies. Yours replace the stone. The game keeps the
movement and the seal.

## What each door must have (all five)

- **One closed door is fine** (owner, 2026-10-18: "your prompt generates the full door closed, can't you just slice
  the door like you did with other assets?"). The lead cuts it in `mi_prep.py`, as with the enemies' parts:
  - the **slab** is everything inside the opening between the jambs, below the lintel, and the game slides it down into
    the floor;
  - the **frame** is the rest, and the cut faces are capped so nothing looks hollow when the slab is down.
  
  The one thing that helps the cut: the slab sits **a little inset** between the jambs, with a clear groove all round
  it, not fused into them.
- **The same silhouette in every biome** so the player always knows "the boss is behind this":
  - square, heavy jambs;
  - a massive lintel that sticks out past the jambs;
  - a horned or skull-like crest on the lintel;
  - one thick slab.
  
  Only the material, small details and accent colour change.
- **A round empty socket in the middle of the slab, on both faces** (about 0.9 m across, a little sunken). The game
  places its own glowing red seal there so it can switch it off when the boss dies. Don't model a glowing seal.
- **The accent colour** is in the carvings, cracks or veins only and should be **faint, not glowing**. The game adds
  the light.
- **Size:** opening 3.0 m wide and 2.2 m tall, the frame about 4.2 × 1.0 × 2.9 m (W × D × H), and the slab about
  3.0 × 0.4 × 2.2 m. The game stretches the width a little: the doors run 2.2–3.4 m wide.
- **Budget:** about 5k triangles for the frame and 1.5k for the slab.

## How they go in

Drop them into `unprocessed_images/` as `boss_door_<biome>.glb` and say so in chat. The lead
prepares them like the other models (`scripts/assets/mi_prep.py`: scaled, simplified, maps 1024). Each biome then
shows its own door, keeping the sink, rise, seal and darkening it has today. The code-made door stays as the
fallback.

## Delivered (2026-10-18), installed in v0.8.0 BD

All five arrived closed, as asked. `scripts/assets/bd_prep.py` cuts each one into its frame (two jambs and the lintel)
and its slab along the groove, caps the cuts, and scales it about 18 % over the code-made door (a 2.6 m slab; owner:
"15-20% bigger"). The slab's width is always the sim's doorway. Each biome's boss room shows its own door. Every door
had the round socket, and the game's red seal sits in it. Numbers and shots:
[`roadmap/v0.8.0/evidence/BOSS_DOORS.md`](../../roadmap/v0.8.0/evidence/BOSS_DOORS.md).
