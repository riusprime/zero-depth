# Art request: Starfall kit pieces (v0.7.5 NB2)

Biome 5, **Starfall** (owner N3: "a fifth one. That's gonna be kind of like a cosmic or universe feel with different
stars and things like that"; the name is a working name, a string the owner may change). It is built today from the
owner's kit (walls and cover tinted dark violet, `rock_large`, the fire props with a violet flame) plus code-made
pieces where the kit has nothing cosmic: star crystals and meteorites on the floor and the star monolith as square
cover (`CodeProps`), the starglow pools (`StarglowViews`), and the starry void past the floor's edge: the floor's
rock underside and its hanging spikes, the stars, the nebula clouds and the floating rock and crystal islands
(`BiomeEdge` + `StarVoid`). The pieces below would replace the code-made ones. Each goes in
`assets/models/kit/<id>.glb` and joins `KitModels.SPECS` and `assets/models/manifest.json`; until then the code-made
piece draws (L15: missing art never blocks a step).

Target look: the owner's kit and [`../look_reference.webp`](../look_reference.webp) (chunky, chipped low-poly pieces
in a dark scene), with the crystal-shard style of the card frames for the crystals (faceted, crisp facet highlights,
a soft inner glow). Palette: dark violet stone (`#3B3358` cover, `#4A4270` rock), lavender-grey ground (`#7E779C`),
crystal pale violet `#E9C8FF` glowing magenta-violet `#C060F0`, nebula violet `#7A3CFF`, teal `#2AB8A8` and magenta
`#E040B0`. Keep off the player-core cyan `#2BC4E2` and the enemy red.

The game tints and lights the pieces itself, so **no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no symbols or runes**
in any image or model.

## Shared style (put this at the start of every prompt)

> Low-poly faceted 3D game prop, stylised, chunky, flat-shaded facets with crisp edges, a single object centred on
> a plain white background, three-quarter view from above, soft even studio light, no text, no letters, no numbers,
> no logo, no symbols, no ground plane, same style as a modular kit of ruined-city props (concrete walls, crate
> stacks, fire barrels) for a dark isometric action roguelike set among the stars.

## 1. `star_crystal` (decoration, about 0.5 × 0.5 m, 0.4 m tall)

> [shared style] A small cluster of three hexagonal crystals growing from one root, the tallest almost upright, two
> smaller ones leaning outward, pointed tips, translucent pale violet crystal with bright facet highlights and a
> magenta-violet glow from inside, a few tiny glowing specks around the base.

## 2. `meteorite` (decoration, about 0.6 × 0.5 m, 0.26 m tall)

> [shared style] A small fallen meteorite, a low lumpy faceted stone, dark violet-grey with a rough pitted surface,
> a thin faint teal glow in two or three cracks, a little scorched dust at its foot.

## 3. `star_monolith` (cover, square footprint about 1 × 1 m, 1.6 m tall)

Joins `rock_large` in Starfall's square cover (`BiomeLook.square_cover`, 1 in 4).

> [shared style] A leaning standing stone of dark violet star rock, five-sided, tapering to an off-centre point,
> broad flat facets, one vein of small pale violet crystals running up one face with a soft glow, no carvings.

## 4. `floating_island` (void prop, about 2 × 2 m top, 2 m deep; optional)

Drifts in the void past the floor's edge (`StarVoid.island_mesh`), seen from above and the side.

> [shared style] A small floating island of dark violet rock, a flat jagged top, its underside narrowing to a rough
> point like an upside-down mountain, loose pebbles under it, a few small pale violet crystals on top.

## 5. `void_spike` (void prop, about 1 × 1 m, 2.5 m long; optional)

Hangs from the floor's rim (`StarVoid.spike_mesh`): the floor reads as one big floating island.

> [shared style] A hanging stalactite of dark violet rock, five uneven faceted sides narrowing to a point, rough
> chipped faces, seen from the side.

## 6. Ground texture `ground_star` (optional, 1024 × 1024, tileable)

Would replace the shared kit ground on Starfall floors (`StageView.GROUND_TEXTURES`).

> Seamless tileable top-down texture of pale lavender-grey star dust over cracked dark stone, scattered tiny
> glittering specks, a few small dark pebbles, soft cool violet tones, even flat lighting with no cast shadows,
> stylised hand-painted look matching low-poly game art, no text, no symbols.

## How it goes in

- The owner generates the models (Tripo or similar) and uploads them with their texture images to the repo root or
  `unprocessed_images/`, as with the kit.
- The lead installs each as `assets/models/kit/<id>.glb`, adds its `KitModels.SPECS` row (yaw, height, role) and
  manifest entry; the code-made piece of the same id stops drawing (`StageKit.piece_of` asks the kit first). The two
  void props (4, 5) would need `StarVoid` to load them by id (a small change, not made yet).
- Until then: `star_crystal`, `meteorite` and `star_monolith` are code-made (`src/presentation/world_view/code_props.gd`);
  the islands, spikes, stars and nebula are code-made (`src/presentation/world_view/star_void.gd`); the ground is the
  shared kit ground tinted with the biome's `ground` colour.
