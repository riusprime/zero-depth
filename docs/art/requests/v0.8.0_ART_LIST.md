# v0.8.0: everything the owner can generate (the full list, 2026-10-14)

Owner: "work as far as you can without generating new models and give me the full list of things you need that I
will be generating this afternoon after work". Every item below has a code-made stand-in today or by the step that
needs it, so nothing waits on art (L15). The list is ordered by what changes the game most. Tick what you deliver.

**Delivery:** drop the `.glb` files (with their textures) and PNGs into `unprocessed_images/`, named by the id.
The lead installs, sizes, lists them in the manifests and checks the asset tests.

**Shared rules (all models):** the look of your kit and [`../look_reference.webp`](../look_reference.webp):
chunky, chipped, weathered low-poly; worn metal, concrete and stone; no baked lighting or shadows; no text, letters
or logos; a single object, no ground plane; readable from an isometric camera about 35° down.

**Kit prefix** (props and room pieces; the same as KIT_REQUESTS):
> Low-poly faceted 3D game prop, stylised, chunky and slightly weathered, flat-shaded facets with crisp edges, a
> single object centred on a plain white background, three-quarter view from above, soft even studio light, no
> text, no letters, no numbers, no logo, no ground plane, same style as a modular kit of ruined-city props (concrete
> walls, crate stacks, a car wreck, fire barrels) for a dark isometric action roguelike.
