## What "the new style" means here (read from the owner's files)

These notes come from the owner's delivered files, not from a written spec. The owner corrects them.

- **The first three boss models:** Tripo-style textured meshes (one baked colour texture each; the Stone Sentinel
  also has a normal map). The look is faceted plates and blocks in **saturated red (about `#C8221E`) and charcoal
  grey (about `#3A3A3E`–`#6A6462`)**, with small bright red emissive accents (visor slots, cracks).
- **The environment rework:** weathered concrete walls and slabs (`#504D4F` mean), rusted metal (a car wreck, a
  fire barrel, a brazier: rust orange-brown about `#4C3E37`–`#8A4A2A`), worn stone floor tiles (`ground-a.png`,
  mean `#7E6750`), dark and desaturated, a ruined city lit by fire.
- **So the bosses should be:** chunky, readable low-poly silhouettes (they are seen small, from a fixed iso camera
  about 35 degrees down), red-and-charcoal like the first three, with worn, scratched, slightly rusted metal and
  stone surfaces instead of clean flat colour, and only a few glowing red-orange parts. Their weak points glow
  **gold** (the game shows a gold core when the weak point opens).

## Hand-off rules (so the model drops straight in)

- One `.glb` per boss, at `assets/models/bosses/<id>.glb` (`warlord`, `hive_lens`, `foundry`, optionally
  `lens_drone`), plus its texture files beside it. The lead adds each to `assets/models/manifest.json` (id, path,
  sha256); the asset test checks it.
- Any scale and facing: `BossModels` normalises each model to its in-game height and turns its front to +X from one
  row per id in `BossModels.SPECS` (yaw, height), which the lead adds from a render of the raw model (as for the first
  three, `docs/roadmap/v0.3.0/evidence/BOSSES.md`). In-game heights: Warlord about 2.7 m, Hive Lens about 1.9 m
  across (the game floats it 1.75 m up, so model it without a stand), Foundry about 2.6 m, Lens Drone about 0.6 m.
- A single mesh is fine: the game moves an unrigged model as a whole body (BossModels / BossRig), as it did with the
  first three. Separate parts (the Warlord's shield and spear, the Foundry's door, the Hive Lens's three pods) are a
  bonus: they could be animated later.
- Up to about 30k triangles each; one 2048 px colour texture (a normal map is optional).

## 1. The Warlord (floor 1 pool)

**Image prompt (reference, for an image generator):**

> A towering armoured knight boss for an isometric action roguelike, 3/4 front view and a side view on a plain
> neutral grey background. About 2.7 m tall on short, heavy armoured legs. Built from chunky faceted plates of dark
> charcoal steel, worn and scratched, with patches of rust at the edges. A closed boxy great-helm with a glowing red
> T-shaped visor slit, crowned by three swept-back red crystal blades. Broad red faceted pauldrons and a tattered red
> tabard front and back. On its left arm a tall red tower shield almost as tall as its body, rimmed in grey iron,
> with a glowing red faceted emblem in the centre. In its right hand a long grey spear twice its height with a red
> leaf-shaped crystal head. Small inset: the shield lifted high and aside, baring a glowing gold core in its chest.
> Low-poly faceted game asset, hand-painted worn textures, red (#C8221E) and charcoal grey (#3A3A3E) palette with
> rust-brown wear, dark ruined-city mood, a few emissive red-orange accents, soft studio light, no text.

**3D model prompt (for a text- or image-to-3D tool, e.g. Tripo, using the image above as input):**

> Game-ready low-poly 3D model of an armoured knight boss, standing pose with the shield on the left arm raised in
> front and a long spear held upright in the right hand, facing forward. Chunky faceted charcoal steel plates with
> worn, scratched, slightly rusted metal; red faceted pauldrons, red tabard, red tower shield with an iron rim and a
> glowing red emblem; closed boxy helm with a glowing red T visor and three red crystal blades on top; red crystal
> spearhead. Hard-surface, stylised, clear silhouette from above, baked PBR colour texture, about 25k triangles,
> single object, no base, no background.
