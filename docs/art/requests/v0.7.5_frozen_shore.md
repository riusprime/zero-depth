# Art request: Frozen Shore kit pieces (v0.7.5 NB1)

Biome 4, **Frozen Shore** (owner N2: "create the new ground, the fourth biome, the frozen shore"). It is built today
from the owner's kit (walls, slate-tinted rocks and dead trees, fire barrels) plus code-made pieces where the kit
has no ice: snow drifts and ice shards on the floor (`CodeProps`), the ice patches (`IceViews`), and the frozen sea,
snow bank and ice floes past the floor's edge (`BiomeEdge`). The pieces below would replace the code-made ones. Each
goes in `assets/models/kit/<id>.glb` and joins `KitModels.SPECS` and `assets/models/manifest.json`; until then the
code-made piece draws (L15: missing art never blocks a step).

Target look: the owner's kit and [`../look_reference.webp`](../look_reference.webp) (chunky, chipped, weathered
low-poly pieces lit by warm fires in a dark scene), with the crystal-shard style of the card frames for the ice
(faceted, crisp facet highlights). Reference for the biome itself: [`../biomes_reference.png`](../biomes_reference.png),
bottom right panel (white snow, dark slate blocks, bare dead trees, dark blue water with white ice floes).

The game tints and lights the pieces itself, so **no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos** in any image or model.

## Shared style (put this at the start of every prompt)

> Low-poly faceted 3D game prop, stylised, chunky and slightly weathered, flat-shaded facets with crisp edges, a
> single object centred on a plain white background, three-quarter view from above, soft even studio light, no
> text, no letters, no numbers, no logo, no ground plane, same style as a modular kit of ruined-city props (concrete
> walls, crate stacks, a car wreck, fire barrels) for a dark isometric action roguelike.

## 1. `snow_drift` (decoration, about 0.9 × 0.6 m, 0.28 m tall)

> [shared style] A low wind-packed snow drift, a long soft mound with a sharp sculpted crest on one side, a few
> broad flat facets, pure white with pale blue shadows in the hollows, a little grit and a couple of small dark
> pebbles poking out at its foot.

## 2. `ice_shard` (decoration, about 0.5 × 0.5 m, 0.4 m tall)

> [shared style] A small cluster of three faceted ice crystals growing from one frozen root, the tallest leaning
> forward, square-sectioned spikes with pointed tips, translucent pale blue-white ice with bright facet highlights
> and a faint inner glow, a crust of snow around the base.

## 3. `ice_rock` (cover, square footprint, about 1.2 m tall; optional)

Would join `rock_large` and `dead_tree` in Frozen Shore's square cover (`BiomeLook.square_cover`).

> [shared style] A large dark slate boulder half buried in a snow drift, its top capped with snow, a cluster of
> faceted pale blue ice crystals breaking out of one side, cracked flat faces, dark blue-grey stone.

## 4. `ice_floe` (water prop, about 2 × 1.6 m, 0.12 m thick; optional)

Floats on the frozen sea past the floor's edge (`BiomeEdge`), drawn flat.

> [shared style] A flat irregular slab of sea ice, six to eight straight broken edges, a thin layer of snow on top
> with a few cracks, the sides showing layered translucent blue ice, seen from above at a steep angle.

## 5. Ground texture `ground_snow` (optional, 1024 × 1024, tileable)

Would replace the shared `ground_b.png` on Frozen Shore floors (`StageView.GROUND_TEXTURES`).

> Seamless tileable top-down texture of packed snow over frozen ground, wind ripples, faint footprints-free
> crust, a few small dark stones and patches of ice showing through, soft cool white and pale blue-grey tones,
> even flat lighting with no cast shadows, stylised hand-painted look matching low-poly game art, no text.

## How it goes in

- The owner generates the models (Tripo or similar) and uploads them with their texture images to the repo root
  or `unprocessed_images/`, as with the kit.
- The lead installs each as `assets/models/kit/<id>.glb`, adds its `KitModels.SPECS` row (yaw, height, role) and
  manifest entry; the code-made piece of the same id stops drawing.
- Until then: `snow_drift` and `ice_shard` are code-made (`src/presentation/world_view/code_props.gd`), `ice_rock`
  is not used (square cover is `rock_large` 3 : `dead_tree` 1), the floes are code-made hexagonal slabs, and the
  ground is the shared kit ground tinted with the biome's snow `ground` colour.
