# Art request: tiling textures for the procedural exterior walls (v0.8.0 N10, owner O66)

Owner, 2026-10-17: the stacked kit pieces on the exterior walls look "cheap on the overall walls, especially when
they get bigger"; walls should be "a model that uses procedural generation … simpler using textures for most of it".
The lead builds one continuous wall mesh per wall run in code (chunky faceted, a ruined top edge, mitred corners);
**these textures cover it**. Nothing waits on them: the Ruins prototype starts from a free CC0 concrete texture, and
yours replace it per biome once you pick the direction (G2).

**Not needed to start.** Make these after you see the Ruins prototype and say "go".

## What each biome needs

| ✓ | id | biome | what it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| ☐ | `tex_wall_ruins` | Ruins | the wall face |
| ☐ | `tex_wall_night_rocks` | Night Rocks | the wall face |
| ☐ | `tex_wall_red_canyon` | Red Canyon | the wall face |
| ☐ | `tex_wall_frozen_shore` | Frozen Shore | the wall face |
| ☐ | `tex_wall_starfall` | Starfall | the wall face |
| ☐ | `tex_wall_top_<biome>` (optional) | each | the broken top edge, seen from above (rubble, moss, snow, crystal) |

## Rules (all of them)

- **Square PNG, 1024 × 1024** (2048 welcome; the lead resizes).
- **Seamless left-right and top-bottom** (tileable): the wall repeats it along its length. If your tool has a
  "tileable" or "seamless" switch, turn it on.
- **Flat, even light, no baked shadows or highlights, no perspective**: a straight-on photo-like view of a flat
  wall surface, as if scanned. The game lights it.
- **No text, letters, logos, posters, windows or doors** (a window that repeats every 2 m looks wrong).
- **Mid-grey to mid-tone values**, not too dark: rooms are dim and the game darkens the base itself.
- Same chunky, weathered style as your kit and `docs/art/look_reference.webp`.

## Prompt prefix

> Seamless tileable texture, flat orthographic front view of a wall surface filling the whole frame, even diffuse
> lighting, no shadows, no perspective, no vignette, no text, no letters, no logo, stylised hand-painted game
> texture, chunky shapes readable from far, for a dark isometric action roguelike,

## Per biome (after the prefix)

- **`tex_wall_ruins`:** a ruined grey concrete wall with exposed red brick patches where the plaster fell off,
  long vertical cracks, rust stains dripping from the top, faded grime, a few chipped holes showing rebar.
- **`tex_wall_night_rocks`:** a dark blue-grey rock cliff face, large faceted blocky planes, thin cracks, patches of
  dry moss and pale lichen, a few small embedded crystals that are not glowing.
- **`tex_wall_red_canyon`:** a layered red and orange sandstone cliff, horizontal strata bands of different
  thickness, wind-worn edges, small pits and dry cracks, dusty.
- **`tex_wall_frozen_shore`:** a dark grey rock wall crusted with ice and packed snow, frozen drips, a pale blue
  frost film over the stone, cracks filled with snow.
- **`tex_wall_starfall`:** dark violet stone blocks with tiny glowing star specks embedded, faint carved lines,
  cracked edges, a cold cosmic feel (the specks small and sparse).
- **`tex_wall_top_<biome>` (optional):** seen from above, the broken top of a wall: crumbled chunks and dust for
  Ruins, moss and pebbles for Night Rocks, sand for Red Canyon, snow for Frozen Shore, small crystals for Starfall.

**Negative prompt (if the tool has one):** text, letters, logo, watermark, window, door, poster, perspective, shadow,
vignette, frame, border, photo of a building, people, plants in front, 3D render with lighting.

## How they go in

Drop the PNGs into `unprocessed_images/` named by id and say so in chat. The lead checks they tile (and fixes the
seams if not, as with TX2's surfaces), installs them under `assets/textures/kit/`, lists them in the manifest and
re-shoots the walls.
