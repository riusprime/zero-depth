## How the images are used
- **Why planes:** the camera is orthographic and looks down at 35°, so there is no sky. What you see around and
  between the floor islands is whatever lies **below** the floor.
- **The layers:** each biome gets 2 horizontal planes far under the floor:
  - **Deep** (−14 m): opaque, the darkest, barely moving;
  - **Mid** (−6 m): a PNG **with transparency**, drifting slowly so it parallaxes against Deep as the camera moves.
- **What you see:** they are seen at an angle and from far away, so big soft shapes read and fine detail is lost.
  Keep them **dark**: the floor and enemies must stay readable on top (the readability test needs ≥ 3:1 contrast with
  the floor).
- **Tiling:** the planes tile, so every image must be **seamless** (tileable on both axes).

## Format (every file)
- PNG, **2048 × 2048**, seamless on both axes, top-down view (no horizon, no perspective, no vanishing point).
- Deep: no transparency. Mid: a transparent background, with soft-edged shapes.
- Value: the brightest areas under ~35 % brightness for Deep, ~60 % for Mid's highlights. No text, no frame, no
  vignette.
- File names exactly as below, dropped into `unprocessed_images/backgrounds/`.
