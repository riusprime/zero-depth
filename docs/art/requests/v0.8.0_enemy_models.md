## The look, in short
- **Like your Charger, Warden and Needle:** chunky faceted plates, worn scratched metal and stone, charcoal grey
  (about `#3A3A3E`–`#6A6462`) plus **the kind's role colour** instead of the old shared red, a few glowing eye or
  vent accents.
- **Role colours (v0.8.0 Q1):** melee rushers red-orange (about `#D8562A`), tanks bronze (about `#A8743A`), support
  green (about `#4FAE6A`), bombers and mines yellow (about `#D8A824`), ranged blue-violet (about `#5A62D8`). The game
  fine-tunes the shade per kind, so a neutral version of the colour is fine.
- **Keep the greys neutral** (biomes tint them). **No baked shadows or lighting.**
- **Readable from above:** a fixed isometric camera about 35° down, enemies seen small; silhouette over detail.
- **Rigged is a bonus** (legs, arms, the shield). Without a rig the lead cuts parts in code as with the Foundry.

## How they go in
- **Delivery:** upload each `.glb` and its textures to `unprocessed_images/` (or the repo root), named by id.
- **The lead:** adds each to `assets/models/manifest.json`, sets its height and facing from a render, checks the asset
  test and swaps the code-made body for your model (v0.8.0 EM). Behaviour doesn't change.
