# Art request: the build picker (v0.6.1 R2)

The start screen where the player picks the **Blade** or the **Gun**. Today it is two code-drawn dark cards
(340 × 470 px each, side by side) under a title. The art should match the owner's crystal card frames
(`docs/roadmap/v0.6.0/refs/card_style_reference.webp`, `card_templates_empty.webp`) and the wide plaques
(`docs/roadmap/v0.6.1/refs/plaque_templates_empty.webp`).

The game draws all text itself, in English and Spanish, so **no letters or numbers in any image**.

## Shared style (put this at the start of every prompt)

> Stylised hand-painted pixel-art game UI asset, faceted low-poly crystal shards with crisp dark outlines and
> bright highlights on each facet, a subtle inner glow, a dark muted inner panel with a thin bevelled border,
> small floating crystal fragments around the edges, clean readable silhouette, no text, no letters, no numbers,
> no watermark, isolated on a plain white background, centred, consistent lighting from the top left, same art
> style as a set of crystal card frames for a dark ruined-city action roguelike.

## 1. Two build card frames (the main pieces)

One image with both, side by side, so they match. Portrait, about 3:4 (the game shows them at 340 × 470).

> [shared style] Two tall portrait card frames side by side, about 3:4 each, larger and more heroic than a normal
> card. The left frame is made of icy pale-cyan and white crystal shards with a few sharp blade-like spikes rising
> from the top, the right frame of warm amber and orange crystal shards with a squared, mechanical, angular crown.
> Each frame surrounds a large empty dark inner panel (the top half for a weapon emblem, the bottom third for
> text). A heavier cluster of crystals at the top centre of each frame, a broken stone base at the bottom, a few
> shards floating off the sides. Inner panels completely empty.

## 2. Two weapon emblems (go inside the frames)

Square, transparent if the generator allows it (otherwise white).

> [shared style] Two square emblems side by side, no frame. Left: a crystal sword, a long faceted blade of
> pale-cyan crystal with a dark stone hilt, angled 45°, with a faint cyan slash arc behind it. Right: a compact
> crystal pistol, an angular dark-metal body with glowing amber crystal coils and a crystal barrel tip, angled
> 45°, with three small amber energy bolts flying off it. Both centred, filling about 80 % of their square.

## 3. Title plaque

Wide, about 4:1. The game writes "Choose your weapon" / "Elige tu arma" in it.

> [shared style] One wide horizontal title plaque, about 4:1, like a crown: a long dark inner panel with a thin
> bevelled border, large crystal clusters at both ends in pale cyan fading to amber (left to right), a small
> crystal spire at the top centre. Inner panel completely empty.

## 4. Background (optional)

1920 × 1080, behind the cards. The game darkens and blurs it, so detail can stay soft.

> Stylised painted dark ruined city plaza at night seen from a high three-quarter view, broken concrete slabs,
> rubble, a dead tree, rusted car wrecks, a few braziers giving warm orange light, and in the centre a huge
> cracked crystal shard growing out of the ground glowing pale cyan, smaller crystal clusters scattered around
> its base, cold moonlight from the top left, deep shadows, muted desaturated palette with the crystal as the
> brightest element, no characters, no text.

## How it goes in

- Pieces 1–3 are cut out like the card frames (`scripts/art/crop_card_frames.py`): background removed, edges
  defringed, a manifest entry (the owner's own art) and an asset test.
- Upload the images to the repo root or `unprocessed_images/`, the same as the kit models.
- Until they arrive, the build picker uses the menus' Cold-glass style (v0.6.1 Step PQ).
