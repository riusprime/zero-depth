# Art request: the arrival altar, the final altar and the floor portals (v0.9.0)

Owner, 2026-10-22 ([`../../roadmap/v0.9.0/feedback/2026-10-22_combat_visual_overhaul.md`](../../roadmap/v0.9.0/feedback/2026-10-22_combat_visual_overhaul.md)
§2): "Replace the current starting portal/platform with a substantially larger, fully modeled 3D altar … Circular
stone platform, approximately 3 times larger … Three or four stone steps … Floating or carved arcane runes … Glowing blue
symbols, with optional small crystals or jewels around the sides … We need a new prompt for this model".

Today the hero arrives on a code-made stand-in, a 2.2 m stone disc with four metal arms
(`src/presentation/world_view/floor_receiver.gd`). Your altar replaces it. The game keeps the arrival sequence and adds
the moving parts itself (PLAN steps SA and FA).

## What the altar must have

- **One model, two colours.** The same altar is the blue arrival altar (floor 1 of a run only; owner Q16) and the
  ominous purple altar of the final approach (§3). Model it in **neutral stone with no glow**: the game lights the
  runes blue or purple.
- **Size:** about **6.6 m across** (3 × today's 2.2 m), the top platform about 4.4 m across, total height about
  1.0 m, with **3–4 steps** all the way round (a stepped round dais, not a cylinder).
- **Carved rune slots, empty.** A ring of 8–12 shallow carved panels on the step faces or the platform rim, with
  simple angular glyphs cut in. The game puts its own glowing glyphs over them and floats more runes above. Don't
  model floating runes; they must be separate so they can orbit.
- **Jewel sockets.** 6–8 small empty cup-shaped sockets around the outer rim. The game places its own crystals there
  so each can light up in turn. Don't model the jewels, or model them as **separate objects** next to the altar.
- **A clear centre.** The middle of the platform is a flat circle about 1.8 m across, with a faint carved ring, where
  the hero appears. Nothing stands on it.
- **Walkable.** No railings or pillars on the top; the hero walks off in any direction.
- **Budget:** about 6k triangles.

## Prompt

Use the **kit prefix** from `docs/OWNER_TODO.md` §3, then:

> a large ancient round stone altar dais seen from above at three-quarter view, a wide circular platform raised on
> three or four broad stepped tiers running all the way round, weathered pale grey faceted stone blocks with chipped
> edges and thin cracks, a ring of shallow carved panels with simple angular rune glyphs on the step faces, small empty
> cup-shaped sockets spaced around the outer rim, a flat smooth central circle with a faint carved ring in the middle of
> the top, no pillars, no railings, no arch, no crystals, no glow, no floating objects

**Negative prompt (if the tool has one):**
> text, letters, logo, glowing runes, light beams, crystals, floating stones, statues, people, pillars, archway,
> background scenery, ground plane, baked shadows, realistic photo

> [kit prefix] a tall narrow upright portal frame shaped like an elongated egg or teardrop, an organic ring of rough

## How they go in

Drop it into `unprocessed_images/` as `arrival_altar.glb` and say so in chat. The lead prepares them like the other models (`scripts/assets/mi_prep.py`: scaled, simplified, maps 1024). The
code-made stand-ins stay as the fallback.
