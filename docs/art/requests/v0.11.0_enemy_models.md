## How to use this

1. **Image step:** paste the **Image prompt** into your image tool. If it takes a reference image, attach
   `docs/art/enemies_visual_reference.png` (your Charger, Warden and Needle). The family look comes from there.
2. **3D step:** give the image you picked to your 3D tool, with the **3D prompt**.
3. **Negative prompt:** if either tool has one, use the shared one below.
4. **Save** as `assets/models/enemies/<id>.glb`, with its texture next to it. Tell the lead and it installs, cuts the
   parts and animates them in code (VAT). A rig is a bonus, never needed.

**Shared negative prompt:**
> text, letters, numbers, logo, watermark, background scenery, ground, floor, base, pedestal, stand, baked shadows,
> baked ambient occlusion, strong rim light, smooth organic skin, fur, realistic photo, clean shiny plastic, cute
> cartoon eyes, many tiny details, thin wires, floating particles, multiple characters

## What every one of them shares (the Charger's family)

- **Head:** a faceted, ridged shell or hood in the role colour on top. Under it, a dark shield-shaped face plate with
  **one** glowing gem visor in the role colour. Never two eyes, never a mouth with teeth (except where a prompt asks).
- **Body:** charcoal steel, worn and scratched (about `#3A3A3E` to `#6A6462`).
- **Limbs:** grey segmented limbs with bone-coloured tips (about `#D9CDB8`).
- **Greys stay neutral.** Each biome tints them; a warm or blue grey fights the tint.
- **Glow** only on the visor and the one feature named in the prompt. Everything else is matte.
- **Read from above:** the camera looks down about 35° from far away, so an enemy is about 60–120 px tall on screen.
  A clear silhouette beats detail. If two of these would look alike as black shapes, one of them is wrong.
- **Proportions:** chunky. Thick plates, few big facets. Low-poly but not blocky; about 5k triangles.

**Sizes.** The footprint is the enemy's real collision circle in the game. The height is a target relative to the
hero, who stands about 1.7 m. The game scales the model to the footprint, so the **proportions** matter more than the
exact metres.

## The variants

The five biome variants (Rubble Warden, Dim Needle, Ember Charger, Frost Charger, Split Sniper) use their base kind's
model with effects the game draws. They don't need models.
