# Cursor sheet prompt (v0.10.5, owner W8)

Owner, 2026-11-05: "help me with a prompt for a matching cursor". One sheet in the card-icon style
([`README.md`](README.md)), for the owner's n8n workflow: `size` 1920x960 (a 4x2 grid of 480 px cells),
`background` transparent, `output_format` png. Save it as `art_raw/sheets/cursors_astral.png`.

The game needs seven states (`src/presentation/ui/game_cursor.gd`), cut to 32, 48 and 64 px. The six pointers share
one hotspot at the tip, so every pointer is drawn the same size, pointing the same way, with its tip in the same
spot of its cell. The aim reticle's hotspot is its centre.

When the sheet arrives, `scripts/art/cut_cursor_sheet.py` gets a grid mode for it (its cells are pixel boxes of the
old v0.9.5 sheet), and the Astral cursors are swapped for it.

## Prompt

```text
Fantasy game mouse-cursor sheet, delivered as a PNG with a fully transparent background (real alpha), no background colour, no white, no checkerboard, no vignette, no glow halo filling the cell edges, nothing touching the cell borders. A grid of 4 columns by 2 rows of equal square cells on that fully transparent background, 8 cells; cells 1 to 7 each hold one cursor, cell 8 is left completely empty and transparent. Every cursor in the sheet shares ONE identical style, scale, light and palette, as if painted by the same artist in one sitting. MEDIUM: painterly digital fantasy illustration for the mouse cursors of Zero Depth, a dark night-time isometric action roguelike of runes, moonlight and ethereal crystal, matching its card icons; NOT pixel art, NOT flat vector, NOT a 3D render, NOT a photo. RENDERING: each cursor is a small object of polished silver and pale ethereal crystal-light, painted with soft brushwork and smooth gradients, with a faint inner glow along its brightest edge; at most two tiny star-like sparkles per cursor, and none on the pointer's tip. LIGHT: one key light from the TOP-LEFT on every cursor: silver-white highlights on the top-left faces and edges, cool deep slate shading on the bottom-right faces. EDGES: crisp, clean, bold silhouettes with a thin dark ink-blue edge (#0E131C) all the way round, so the cursor reads on both a dark stone floor and a pale silver panel; never a thick cartoon outline, never a white sticker outline, never a fuzzy or smeared edge. GLOW: very restrained: a soft halo no wider than about 3% of the cell, around the brightest parts only; never a bloom, never a glow that tints the background, no lens flares. PALETTE (use only these): silver #E6ECF4 for the lit surfaces, moon-blue #8FB8E8 for mid-tones and the glow, ice highlight #CFE4FF for the hottest highlights, deep slate shadow #1B2433 for the shadowed faces, ink #0E131C for the darkest edges; only cell 6 adds a muted rose #D9667A and only cell 7 is greyed, as those cells say. READABILITY: each cursor is shrunk to 32x32 px in the game and must still read at that size: one bold simple silhouette, no thin filigree, no fine detail that turns to noise when small. POINTER SHAPE (cells 1 to 4, 6 and 7): a slim faceted crystal-and-silver arrowhead, like a carved silver rune-dart or a narrow shard of moonstone, pointing UP and to the LEFT at 45 degrees, its very sharp tip a single clear point; a short tapered tail toward the lower right; the same outline, size, angle and position in every pointer cell, so the cursors line up when they swap. PLACEMENT: in every pointer cell the tip sits at about 17% from the cell's left edge and 8% from its top edge, and the whole dart fills about 60% of the cell, leaving a transparent gutter of at least 5% on every side. The aim cursor (cell 5) is centred in its cell and fills about 60% of it. BACKGROUND: fully transparent (a real alpha channel), no white, no grey, no checkerboard, no colour wash, no grid lines. DO NOT DRAW: any background; no text, letters, numbers, runes that look like writing, labels, captions, UI frames, borders, plates or circles behind a cursor, drop shadows, cast shadows, hands, gloves, fingers, faces, gold, warm orange, red, green or purple (except cell 6's rose), photo textures, watermarks, signatures. Cells in reading order (left to right, top to bottom): 1) DEFAULT: the plain pointer dart at rest, silver body, a calm moon-blue inner glow; 2) HOVER: the same dart, a little brighter, its moon-blue inner glow stronger and one small sparkle beside its tail, showing that what is under it can be clicked; 3) CLICK: the same dart pressed, the same outline and tip position, its body a shade darker and its edge glow brighter ice-white, as if pushed in, with a tiny ring of light just at the tip; 4) INTERACT: the same dart with a small hollow silver diamond-shaped gem floating just below and to the right of its tail, the gem glowing softly moon-blue, meaning "use this" for doors, altars and chests; 5) AIM: a circular aiming reticle, a thin silver ring with four short inward ticks at top, bottom, left and right and a tiny bright ice-white dot exactly in the centre, the ring open and light so the game behind stays visible; 6) INVALID: the same pointer dart, its glow turned muted rose #D9667A instead of moon-blue, with a small rose slash-circle (a ring with one diagonal bar) floating just below and to the right of its tail, meaning "not allowed"; 7) DISABLED: the same pointer dart, desaturated slate-grey with no glow and no sparkles, its silhouette and ink edge still clear, meaning "not available"; 8) EMPTY: nothing, fully transparent.
```
